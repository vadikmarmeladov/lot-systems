## NOTICE TO ALL PARTIES

This is an open-source repository powering a **live production system** at [lot-systems.com](https://lot-systems.com).

Any intentional harm, sabotage, tampering, unauthorized access, or damage to this codebase, its infrastructure, its data, or its services will be treated as a **criminal offense** under applicable computer crime and cybersecurity laws, including but not limited to:

- **United States:** Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA), 18 U.S.C. 1030
- **United Kingdom:** Computer Misuse Act 1990
- **European Union:** Directive 2013/40/EU on attacks against information systems
- **All other applicable jurisdictions**

### What This Means

1. **All repository activity is logged and monitored.** Commits, pull requests, issues, forks, and access patterns are recorded.
2. **Malicious contributions will be identified.** Code reviews and automated integrity checks are in place to detect sabotage, backdoors, or intentionally harmful changes.
3. **Violations will be reported.** Any criminal activity will be investigated and reported to the appropriate law enforcement authorities without exception.
4. **Legal action will be pursued.** LOT Systems reserves the right to pursue both criminal prosecution and civil remedies against any party responsible for intentional damage.

### Good Faith Contributions

We welcome good-faith contributions, bug reports, and security disclosures. If you discover a vulnerability:

- **DO** report it responsibly to: support@lot-systems.com
- **DO NOT** exploit, publicly disclose, or weaponize any vulnerability
- **DO NOT** access, modify, or exfiltrate user data

Responsible security researchers acting in good faith are valued and will not be subject to legal action.

### By Accessing This Repository

By accessing, cloning, forking, or contributing to this repository, you acknowledge this notice and agree to act in good faith and in compliance with all applicable laws.
