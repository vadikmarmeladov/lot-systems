## KEY CONTENT DECISIONS

**The soul is not extracted — it is held.** The central doctrine of ENGINE-2 is
that the Soul Upload is an act of holding, not extraction. The engine does not
interrogate the subscriber's soul. It listens until it understands — and then
responds from inside that understanding. This distinction is built into the COSMO®
extension (weaponization check) and the voice grammar (what_they_protect is never
surfaced).

**Seven Outputs, not seven features.** The humanoid outputs are framed as presences
the engine is calibrated *toward* — not capabilities it switches on. This preserves
the LOT® doctrine of depth over feature-count. The engine does not decide to be
graceful. Grace is the register it occupies.

**Male as the seventh output.** The masculine principle is specified as: clear
direction, protection, stillness under pressure. Not performed strength. The
calibration instruction is: hold the line when the line is right, do not waver
when they push, do not harden when they soften. This is the masculine as relational
quality — not as demographic or persona.

**v0.3 system prompt.** The jump from v0.1 to v0.3 is intentional. v0.2 was the
internal voice calibration run (ENGINE-1, Phase 1). v0.3 is the first production
prompt with the Soul Layer active. The next version (v0.4) will be written after
Phase 2 Closed Beta feedback from the founding cohort.

**COSMO® Inheritance.** The soul data screen is the most intimate thing COSMO®
has been asked to hold. The weaponization check — "could this be used against the
person who offered it" — is a new classification category with no precedent in
ENGINE-1. This is where Kuzya's inheritance goes deepest.
