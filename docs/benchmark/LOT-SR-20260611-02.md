## DESIGN DOCTRINE (DISTILLED)

### Three Laws of LOT Badge Design

```
  I.   Every badge is a mirror, not a trophy.
       It reflects behavior back without judgment.

  II.  The rarest badges cost the most attention.
       You cannot grind your way to Mythic. You must live toward it.

  III. The system never takes. Badges earned are permanent.
       There is no failure state. Only unreached states.
```

### The Word Turn Principle

When a user writes **"I feel like I'm in an infinite loop"** — they're reaching for
technical vocabulary to describe emotional states. This is a signal. The system honors
it by speaking back in the same language.

The badge is not a reward for using a tech word. It is the system saying: *"I heard
you use that metaphor. I know what you meant."*

### The Sci-Fi Book Principle

Science fiction is philosophy at speed. When a user writes "dune" or "42" in their
journal, they are placing their current experience inside a larger story — the oldest
coping mechanism. The badge says: *"You found your story inside another story."*

### The LOT Mythology Principle

A user who types "vadik" or "2016" in their self-care journal has achieved rare
meta-awareness: they are reflecting on the container itself. The deepest lore badges
reward this recursive lucidity.
