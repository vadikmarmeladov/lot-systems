## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY — v14 ADDITIONS

The v14 expansion follows four principles:

1. **ESCALATION WITHOUT INFLATION**
   Every new badge type earns its place by covering territory not yet claimed.
   Word Turn v5 targets philosophical vocabulary (not covered by v1-v4).
   Time v5 targets mathematical beauty (tau, palindromes, sequences).
   Calendar v4 targets the nerd calendar (programmer holidays, Lovelace's birthday).

2. **DEPTH UNLOCKS**
   The new mastery tier (v4) requires 2x the thresholds of v3:
   - v3: 1,000 check-ins → v4: 2,000 check-ins
   - v3: 500-word story → v4: 1,000-word story
   Operators who reach v4 mastery are genuinely rare.

3. **SECRET = TRULY HIDDEN**
   "I am LOT," "Malibu," and Perfect Month are all hidden from
   all in-app documentation. They must be community-discovered.
   This maintains the magic of the easter egg system.

4. **RPG NARRATIVE ARC**
   Achievement v2 fills the mid-game gap. Between v1's "first steps"
   achievements and the legendary mastery badges, the new Story Arcs
   give operators clear narrative progression milestones.
