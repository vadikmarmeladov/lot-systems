07 // DOCTRINE NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The v2 expansion deepens the RPG/Arcade self-care doctrine with:

1. WORD TURNS as gameplay — Writing is the interface. The system listens.
   Every word is a potential unlock. The user is always playing without
   knowing the full ruleset. Curiosity and honesty are both rewarded.

2. COSMO® CROSSOVER — The Cosmic Twin badge bridges LOT and COSMO as
   a shared universe. Writing "COSMO" in LOT activates a crossover event.
   This is the only badge that requires knowledge of both systems.

3. TIME AS RITUAL — The new time easter eggs (pi_hour, error_hour,
   sequence_time, lot_hour) reward users who notice the clock. Attention
   to time is a form of presence. Presence is the whole practice.

4. ARCADE COMBOS — XP multipliers make consistent full-day engagement
   feel like achieving a high score. The Perfect Day (x7) is a daily
   boss encounter. Every day offers it. Most days you don't. That's OK.

5. SECRET BOSS TIER — The Infinite (1,000 Qs) and Cosmic Status (10 years)
   are aspirational landmarks. Nobody expects to reach them. They exist so
   the horizon never disappears.

"Self-care is not a quest you complete. It is a world you build." — LOT DOCTRINE
