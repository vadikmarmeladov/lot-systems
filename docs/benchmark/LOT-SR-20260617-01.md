## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY — v15 ADDITIONS

The v15 expansion is organized around one concept: **The Becoming Lexicon**.

v14's words were about the dramatic arc — phoenix, forge, voyage.
v15's words are about the interior process — surrender, anchor, threshold.

Four principles govern the v15 expansion:

1. **THE VERB OF TRANSFORMATION**
   Word Turn v6 selects words that don't describe change from the outside.
   They enact it. "Surrender" is not a noun — it's something you do.
   "Threshold" is not a place — it's a moment. "Emerge" is not past tense.
   These words appear in people's writing *while they are in it*.

2. **PATTERN AS DEVOTION**
   time_anchor (same hour, 14 days) and phoenix_streak (rebuild after break)
   both recognize patterns that cannot be faked. You can't fake 14 days of
   same-hour check-ins. You can't fake rebuilding — the system saw you break.
   These badges recognize the architecture of real habit.

3. **TIME AS THE FINAL RESOURCE**
   five_years (COSMIC tier) introduces a new rarity class that no mastery badge
   or combo chain can match. It requires only one thing: remaining. This is the
   deepest possible acknowledgment that longevity is itself an achievement.

4. **THE INVISIBLE HANDSHAKE**
   key_code ("0451") is the most specific secret boss yet. It requires gaming
   literacy — System Shock, Deus Ex, Bioshock all share this code. Any operator
   who types it is speaking a language that predates LOT. The badge speaks back.

## ARCADE SYSTEM UPDATE

v15 adds depth to the existing combo system:

```
deep_scribe:     Journal >= 500 chars → triggers on write, stacks with combo
phoenix_streak:  Streak rebuilt → +1 XP bonus on every Perfect Day for 7 days
time_anchor:     14-day same-hour ritual → activates "RITUAL LOCK" UI indicator
```

Critical Hit / Epic Hit system unchanged from v14:
- > 500 chars memory answer: Critical Hit (+50 XP)
- > 1,000 chars memory answer: Epic Hit (+100 XP)

New COSMIC tier rationale:
- Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythic → Ultra-Rare → Cosmic
- Cosmic is earned through time, not skill. It cannot be optimized.
- Only five_years and future time-based achievements will use this tier.
