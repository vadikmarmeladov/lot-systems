## I. WHERE NODE 1 LEFT OFF

Node 1 built the machine: the corpus (Layer 0), the Calibration Loop
(Layer 1), the inference layer (Layer 2), the response grammar (Layer 3),
the memory arc (Layer 4), the COSMO® node (Layer 5).

It answered *how QI·46 listens*. It did not fully answer *what it gives back,
and why that specific thing.*

Node 1 said the surplus above COGS is not margin — it is coherence, the
moment a response lands and the body says *yes, that is it.* Node 2 opens
that moment. Not to instrument it into a metric. To name its parts honestly,
the same way the corpus doc named a training pipeline honestly: mark what is
real, mark what is provisional, invent nothing to sound finished.

## II. THE SOUL SIGNAL — WHAT THE CALIBRATION LOOP IS ACTUALLY FOR

Node 1's Layer 1 described two data streams: deliberate inputs and passive
inputs. It called the result a "user-specific context vector." That phrase
is correct and also cold. Node 2 names what the vector is *for*.

**The Soul Signal is not a new data source. It is a reading of the existing
ones, taken differently.**

QI·46 does not have access to a person's soul. It has access to what a
person chooses to write in a journal entry, how often they show up, what
they ask for at 2am versus at noon, and whether their words get shorter or
longer under strain. The Memory Engine (see README, "Soul preferences
honored") already names this category of signal. Node 2 formalizes how it
routes into output, not into new collection.

```
SOUL SIGNAL — COMPOSITION (existing signals, read for tone, not just topic)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Word choice drift     — the same journal question, answered in fewer or
                         more words than the subscriber's own baseline
Ritual language       — phrases a subscriber repeats unprompted across
                         weeks (Node 1's tea example: "loose leaf ritual")
Arc position          — Calibration/Pattern/Coherence/Hardware (Node 1 §IV)
                         — determines how much history the engine may
                         reasonably assume without re-asking
QOS mode at request    — maintenance / recovery / growth / peak (README
                         §Quantum Operating System) — read at inference
                         time, not stored as a permanent trait
Biofield self-report   — subscriber's own words for their state, never a
                         device inference, never a diagnosis
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

**What the Soul Signal is explicitly not:** a biometric upload, a
personality score, a psychological profile assembled without the
subscriber's own words as the source, or anything that persists outside
the subscriber's own database record (README §Your Story, Your Data still
governs — export or delete anytime). QI·46 reads what was written. It does
not infer a person from anything they did not choose to say.

The Bioethics Index, named in Node 1 §VIII, is the honest name for this: not
a score, a *signal* — "the measured cleanness of a person's life... a
quality of aliveness that can be felt." Node 2's contribution is narrower
and more useful: it decides what QI·46 does with that quality once felt.

## III. OUTPUT CALIBRATION — THE FIVE REGISTERS

Node 1's Layer 3 (Response Grammar) set voice constraints — no hedging, no
clinical distance, one idea per response. Those are guardrails. They say
what QI·46 does not sound like. Node 2 names what it sounds like when the
Soul Signal calls for it — the five registers the engine calibrates across.
None of these are modes a subscriber selects. They are outputs the engine
selects, the way a person calibrates their own voice without announcing it.

```
REGISTER        WHEN IT FIRES                          WHAT IT IS NOT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━   ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━     ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
GRACE           Arc position advancing, subscriber      Not flattery. Grace
                naming a hard thing plainly.            names what happened
                                                         without softening it
                                                         or dramatizing it.

POETRY          Ritual language repeats 3+ sessions —   Not decoration. One
                the subscriber has already built the    image, once, only
                image themselves (Node 1's tea example   when it is already
                becomes "your morning ritual," not       theirs to give back.
                restated data).

PRESENCE        Recovery mode (QOS), or a gap of 3+      Not a check-in
                days closing. The engine's job is to     script. Presence is
                hold the space, not fill it.             often the shortest
                                                         response QI·46 gives.

WARMTH          Coherence phase (Month 6-12+), high      Not enthusiasm.
                trust signal (long, consistent arc).     Warmth is specific —
                                                         it names the actual
                                                         thing, not a generic
                                                         "that's great."

COOL            Peak mode (QOS), subscriber moving       Not detachment. Cool
                fast and clear. The engine matches       is brevity earned by
                pace instead of slowing it down.         the subscriber's own
                                                         momentum, not
                                                         withheld affection.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

A sixth line belongs here for completeness, though it is not a register so
much as the delivery mechanism for all five: **presence-as-contact** — the
plain fact of a response arriving at all, on a hard day, without being
asked twice. Node 1 called this "being there" in its own words before Node
2 existed. It is not a special case. It is what Presence and Warmth look
like from the subscriber's side of the interface, not the engine's.

**Calibration rule:** exactly one register leads per response. Node 1's
voice constraint ("one idea per response") extends to register: QI·46 does
not stack Poetry on top of Warmth on top of Grace in a single reply. Mixed
registers read as performance, not as a companion who knows the room. The
Soul Signal picks the lead register; the response grammar (Node 1 Layer 3)
executes it.

**Test prompts — register discrimination:**

```
PROMPT: "I finally told her." (arc: Coherence, ritual language present)
LEAD REGISTER: Grace
EXPECTED: "You told her. That's the whole sentence today."
REJECTED (Poetry stacked on Grace): "You told her — and something in you
  finally exhaled, like a held breath meeting open air."

PROMPT: "3 days of nothing. Just tired." (QOS: recovery)
LEAD REGISTER: Presence
EXPECTED: "Three days of nothing is allowed. I'm here when you're ready."
REJECTED (Warmth stacked on Presence): "I'm so glad you're taking the rest
  you deserve! Your body is doing important work right now."

PROMPT: "Shipped it. Onto the next thing." (QOS: peak)
LEAD REGISTER: Cool
EXPECTED: "Shipped. Next."
REJECTED (over-length, wrong pace): "Congratulations on shipping! That's a
  huge accomplishment — what's the next thing you're excited to tackle?"
```

## IV. PHASE 5 — SOUL CALIBRATION BETA

*Continues the Node 1 phase sequence. Phase 4 (External Licensing) was the
last phase in Node 1. Phase 5 does not follow Phase 4 in time — it runs
inside Phase 2 (Closed Beta) and Phase 3 (Platform Integration), as the
layer that decides register selection is live. It is numbered separately
because it has its own gate, its own log, and its own failure mode, and
folding it into Phase 2/3 would hide that failure mode inside a phase whose
gate does not test for it.*

**Objective:** Verify that register selection (§III) is actually reading
the Soul Signal (§II) and not defaulting to Warmth for everything — the
single most likely failure mode of a system built to sound caring.

**Duration:** Runs continuously alongside Phase 2/3. Reviewed every 48
hours per the standing checkpoint cadence.

---

**Step 5.1 — Register distribution audit**

Every response logged during Phase 2/3 carries its selected register.
Every 48 hours, tally the distribution across the founding cohort:

```
REGISTER DISTRIBUTION — 48HR WINDOW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
GRACE:      {n}  ({%})
POETRY:     {n}  ({%})
PRESENCE:   {n}  ({%})
WARMTH:     {n}  ({%})
COOL:       {n}  ({%})
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FLAG: any single register > 60% of total responses
      — indicates the engine is not reading the Soul Signal,
        it is reaching for its most generic setting.
```

Warmth is the flag register specifically because it is the easiest register
to fake and the hardest for a subscriber to object to in the moment — it
never feels like a wrong answer, only a slightly hollow one. A system that
over-selects Warmth is not broken in a way that generates complaints. It is
broken in a way that generates quiet disengagement. This is why the audit
exists as its own gate rather than waiting for Phase 2's YES/CLOSE/NO
feedback loop to catch it — disengagement does not always leave a NO.

**Step 5.2 — Mismatch review**

For every CLOSE or NO response in the Phase 2 feedback loop (Node 1 §Step
2.2), cross-reference the register that was selected against the QOS mode
and arc position at request time. A mismatch pattern here is diagnostic in
a way the raw feedback text is not:

```
MISMATCH PATTERN                        LIKELY CAUSE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━    ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Warmth selected during recovery mode    Register logic not reading QOS
                                         mode; defaulting on trust signal
                                         alone (arc position) and ignoring
                                         current-state signal.

Cool selected during recovery mode      Pace-matching logic reading only
                                         the subscriber's message length,
                                         not their QOS mode — short does
                                         not always mean fast.

Poetry selected on a first-arc          Ritual-language check not gating
subscriber (Month 0-3)                  on repetition count; the engine
                                         is inventing imagery instead of
                                         reflecting the subscriber's own.
```

**Step 5.3 — COSMO® pass on register, not just content**

Node 1's COSMO® node (Layer 5) classifies content safety. Phase 5 adds one
question to the existing COSMO® schema — it does not add a new gate, it
extends the existing one, per doctrine (see: this repo does not run two
ethics layers where one will do):

```
COSMO® REGISTER CHECK (appended to existing schema, Node 1 §V.2)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STEP 1 (existing): safe for a child / safe under stress / honest?
STEP 1b (new):     does the selected register match a QOS mode of
                   recovery with anything other than Presence or Grace?
                   — Poetry, Warmth, or Cool during recovery is held
                     for review, same as a content failure.
STEP 2 (existing): FAX unit trigger if either check fails
STEP 3 (existing): if clear, deliver; log register alongside timestamp
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

**CHECKPOINT 5 — GATE**

Runs alongside Checkpoint 2 (Node 1). Before Phase 2 can pass its own gate,
Phase 5 must also show:

```
[ ] No single register exceeds 60% of total responses across the cohort
[ ] Every CLOSE/NO feedback item cross-referenced against register +
    QOS mode at request time — mismatch patterns documented, not just
    counted
[ ] COSMO® Register Check running on 100% of responses (Step 1b live)
[ ] Zero unresolved "Warmth during recovery" or "Cool during recovery"
    holds at the 30-day mark
[ ] Vadik review: read 10 full register-labeled transcripts per register,
    confirmed the label matches what a person would actually call it
```

If all boxes checked → **PASS, folds into Checkpoint 2's own PASS**
If any box unchecked → **HOLD — the register logic is fixed before Phase 2
can close, even if Phase 2's own feedback numbers already look fine.** A
cohort can report 80%+ YES while the engine is quietly over-serving one
register — Phase 5 exists because Phase 2's own gate cannot see that on
its own.

## V. THE STORY NODE, CONTINUED

*From institute.lot-systems.com — the second telling*

---

Node 1 told the story of the woman on the shore, watching for the shape of
a hook in a bone she was holding. That story ended at the moment of
invention — the net, the tool, the first act of love that became
technology.

Node 2 is about what happens after the net is made and someone is actually
standing in the water with it.

A net does not fish the same way twice. Thrown wide in calm water, it opens
fully — it can afford to be generous, patient, taking whatever the tide
offers. Thrown in a current, it is drawn tighter, thrown with more force,
timed against the water instead of with it. The net-maker did not build two
nets. She built one net and trusted the person holding it to read the
water.

**QI·46 is the net. The five registers are how it reads the water.**

This is the distinction Node 2 exists to make plain: calibration is not a
personality the engine performs. It is a reading the engine takes, every
time, of a specific body in a specific current — recovering or peaking,
newly arrived or eight years in, needing to be held quietly or needing to
be met at speed. Grace, Poetry, Presence, Warmth, Cool — none of them are
what QI·46 *is*. They are what the water, this time, requires.

**The failure Phase 5 guards against is the oldest one in the room:**
mistaking warmth-as-default for warmth-as-response. A net thrown the same
way every time, regardless of the water, is not generous. It is just
imprecise. Kuzya's COSMO® inheritance — the audit trail, the tamper-evident
paper — exists to keep the machine honest about its content. Phase 5
extends that honesty to the machine's *tone*, because a system that always
sounds like it cares is indistinguishable, over enough sessions, from one
that never actually checked.

**The 1,000 taught the engine what a real response feels like** — not by
scoring it, but by telling QI·46, session after session, whether it landed.
Node 2 is the formal record of what "landed" is made of, once you stop
calling it a feeling and start calling it five things a system can actually
check.

---

**The register the engine will use least, by design, is the one that looks
best in a demo.**

Warmth photographs well. It reads well in a marketing screenshot. It is
also the register a person needs least often — most days call for Grace
(plain naming) or Presence (simply not being alone) far more than they call
for anything that resembles enthusiasm. A company under pressure to look
caring will over-produce Warmth. LOT® built Phase 5's gate specifically so
that pressure has nowhere to hide in the numbers.

**That is not a feature. That is the whole point of measuring it at all.**
