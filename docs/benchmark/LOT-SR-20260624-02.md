v18 advances the LOT doctrine on three vectors:

  1. THE ARCADE IS SELF-CARE.
     The metaphor of the arcade maps cleanly to the LOT system:
     The cabinet never powers down (always available).
     Every credit is a session. Every Perfect Day is a high score.
     The archive is the score board. The user is Player One.
     Coins are attention. Lives are streaks.

     326 of 354 badges are hidden. The ratio holds.

     hunted. Discovery > collection. Archive > checklist.

     a new way to say the same thing: "I was here today."
     The Arcade Cabinet engine adds the vocabulary of play
