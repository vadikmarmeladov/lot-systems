## DESIGN NOTES — ORACLE ARCHIVE THEME

### Philosophy of v24

The word turn engine v15 was designed around a single principle:
**words that belong equally to a fantasy spell book and a software manual.**

- "oracle" — used in databases (Oracle DB) and in divination
- "calibrate" — used in engineering and in self-assessment
- "sync" — used in software and in human connection
- "pulse" — used in electronics and in physiology
- "cascade" — used in CSS and in waterfalls

This is the core of LOT's aesthetic: the technical IS the personal.
The RPG IS the operating system. The badge IS the signal.

### Secret Boss v14 Design

Three sci-fi/lit references, deliberately obscure, rarity by design:

1. **THE ANSWER (42)** — Douglas Adams, *Hitchhiker's Guide*. The most famous
   answer to the most famous question. Rarity: RARE. Not rare enough that no one
   finds it, but specific enough to reward the right readers.

2. **SELDON PLAN (Seldon)** — Isaac Asimov, *Foundation*. Hari Seldon and
   psychohistory: the idea that patterns in human behavior are predictable at
   scale. LOT is a personal psychohistory engine. MYTHIC — because not everyone
   has read Foundation, and that's exactly how it should be.

3. **BIG CRUNCH (heat death)** — cosmological entropy. The final state. Anyone
   who thinks about the heat death of the universe while doing self-care gets
   this badge. EPIC.

### LEET HOUR (13:37) — The Only Fully Hidden Time Badge

Every other time badge is mentioned or hinted in UI.
13:37 is not. It appears in no list. It must be discovered.
1337 = LEET in hacker/gamer culture. For those who show up at the right moment.
