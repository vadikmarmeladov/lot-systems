     (behavioral /api/logs vs. quantum-signal /api/quantum-intent/sync), not
     one unified channel. Flagged here as a FINDING for S-2 to confirm this
     split is intentional — if a single unified activity log is the goal,
     that is a real design decision, not a bug fix, and out of scope for an
     unattended session to make unilaterally.
