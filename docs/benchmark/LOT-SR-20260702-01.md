06 // DESIGN NOTES — ORACLE ENGINE RATIONALE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ORACLE AS SELF-CARE FRAME:
  The oracle tradition, stripped of its mysticism, is a technology
  for self-examination. You bring a question. You attend to patterns.
  You receive — not a prophecy — but a clearer picture of what you
  already know but haven't articulated. That is also what check-ins do.

  "oracle" badge trigger: "the answer is always inside" — because
  the check-in is always already a kind of divination. The user
  already knows what they need. The archive listens.

WORD SELECTION PHILOSOPHY:
  oracle / rune / sigil — shapes and systems that hold compressed meaning.
  Same as a well-named badge. Same as a check-in. Dense symbols.
  
  cipher — not secrecy but decoding. You contain the key.
  augur — reading signs. Ancient technology. Still applicable.
  axiom — what you no longer have to prove. Your foundation beliefs.
  vestige — what remains after change. Traces are meaningful.
  covenant — an agreement with yourself. Self-care is a covenant.
  prophesy — to speak what you see. Not to predict, to articulate.

ASCII SYMBOL CHOICES:
  ◇→✦   Diamond-to-star: potential becoming clear
  ≋·≋   Wave-dot-wave: patterns in water / time
  ◈·◈   Faceted diamonds: clarity through cutting
  ↑◇↑   Rising diamond: invocation / call upward
  ○—≋—○ Circles linked by wave: cipher/connection
  ✦·—·✦ Stars across dash: augury reading flight paths
  ∥·○·∥ Parallel lines framing circle: covenant/seal
  ◌·◌·◌ Open circles: arcane / hidden but patterned
  ·—·—· Dots and dashes: vestige / trace / morse
  ═══◇═══ Triple equals framing diamond: axiom / bedrock
  [◇]   Bracketed diamond: glyph / marked symbol
  ◇·✦·◇ Diamond-star-diamond: prophecy / seeing
