## DESIGN NOTES

### Word Turn v7 — WHY ROGUE VOCABULARY

The 6 previous Word Turn engines map an inner journey:
  v1 emotional → v2 sci-fi self → v3 digital → v4 body
  → v5 philosophy → v6 becoming

v7 adds the arc that completes the game metaphor:
The player who knows care is often a dungeon run —
not a meditation retreat. You loot your own insights.
You grind the habits. You save your progress.
You respawn when the streak breaks.

LOT is an RPG. v7 makes that explicit in the word layer.

### Secret Boss: DEEP THOUGHT (42 days)

References The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy.
The 42-day streak is not a round number — it is a signal.
Users who reach it will wonder if it is intentional.
It is.

### Secret Boss: FOUNDER'S GUARD

Requires checking in on April 7 (LOT founding day) for 3 years.
Almost impossible. Requires the user to exist in LOT long enough
to see three April 7ths. Temporal loyalty at its maximum.
Not documented in-app. Must be discovered.

### Secret Boss: VOID MASTER

Writing "void" in 5 different entries triggers this badge.
"void/empty" is already a v3 Word Turn word (INTO THE VOID).
A user who returns to that word five times has something to say
about absence. The badge names it.
