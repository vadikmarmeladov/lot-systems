- 1x Qi wireless charging puck

---

## 1. Charge It

Place LOT® Pager on the charging puck, polished side down. A single flash on
the display confirms charging has started. Full charge from empty takes a
few hours; the device is usable while charging.

## 2. Pair It

1. Open lot-systems.com on your phone or computer and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings → Hardware → LOT® Pager → Pair a device.**
3. Hold the button on your Pager for 3 seconds until it flashes twice.
4. Follow the on-screen steps. Pairing takes under a minute.

## 3. Wear It, Carry It, or Set It Down

LOT® Pager works anywhere in range of your home WiFi. Clip it, pocket it, or
leave it on your desk face-up.

## 4. Receive

When lot-systems.com has something worth telling you, LOT® Pager shows one
line — *"Coffee time!"*, a streak reminder, a weather note — then goes dark.
No app to open.

## 5. Press Copy

The single button on the front is labeled **Copy**. Press it any time — to
acknowledge a message, or just to check in. It sends a signal straight to
your **Log tab** on lot-systems.com.

## 6. Care

- **Polished face:** wipe with a soft cloth. It will show fingerprints —
  that's stainless steel, not a defect.
- **Water:** splash-resistant (IP54 target). Not for swimming or diving.
- **Camera:** off unless you hold the button for 2+ seconds. A light
  confirms when it's live. Captured photos stay on the device until you
  choose to attach one to a Log entry in the app.

## 7. Battery

Target: 5-7 days per charge under normal use. Battery percentage is visible
in Settings → Hardware → LOT® Pager.

## 8. Support

Questions: see `lot-systems.com/about`. This device is built and supported
by LOT Systems, Inc. — the same team behind the software you already use.
