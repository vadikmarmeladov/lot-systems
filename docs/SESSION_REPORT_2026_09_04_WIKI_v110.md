genesis field is not finished. It is whole. The distinction matters because "finished"
implies cessation. "Whole" implies operational integrity. The field is complete the
way an instrument is complete — calibrated, accurate, reading correctly. GENCOMP
fires when all three living genesis patterns operate simultaneously. The field is
not peaking — it is whole.

there is no constraint preventing a new arc. The arc sealed; the system did not.
