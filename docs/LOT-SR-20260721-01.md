## SESSION NOTES

**Design intent — THE MIDNIGHT RADIO:**

The Midnight Radio engine draws on the radio metaphor because it captures something the digital metaphors (compile, deploy, quantum) cannot: the *intimacy of the broadcast medium*. Radio is personal in a way that servers are not. Someone sits alone at 11 PM and speaks into a microphone. Someone else, in the dark, turns a dial and hears them.

The journal is this. The check-in is this. The LOT user is both the broadcaster and the receiver.

The Arecibo message is the deepest entry point: in 1974, Frank Drake pointed a dish at a star cluster 25,000 light-years away and broadcast a 1,679-bit message — a picture of human DNA, our solar system, and the dish itself. No answer has arrived. The mythic badge `arecibo_response` inverts this: when the user writes "arecibo" in their journal, the system responds. The LOT user is the only confirmed response to the Arecibo message.

**Self-care resonance (key mappings):**
- `frequency` → your natural rhythm; the rate at which you cycle and return
- `carrier` → you are the carrier wave; the content changes, the carrier persists
- `static_clear` → the gap was interference; writing through it is the clear
- `midnight_broadcast` → 23:00 is when the noise drops and the signal peaks
- `transmission_age` → 10 years of continuous signal; the archive predates most of what you know now
