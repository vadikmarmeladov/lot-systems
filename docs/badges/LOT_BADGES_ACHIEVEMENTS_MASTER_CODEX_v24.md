### THE ORACLE ARCHIVE — DESIGN NOTES v24

v24 introduces THE ORACLE ARCHIVE engine with a deliberate philosophy:
the oracle is not a prediction engine. It is a pattern-recognition system.

The 12 words (oracle, rune, prophecy, scroll, amplify, relay, encrypt,
pulse, cascade, converge, sync, calibrate) were chosen because they bridge
the ancient and the technical. Every word belongs equally to a fantasy RPG
and to a software manual. This is the core of LOT's language design.

The three Secret Boss badges follow the "library for the reader" principle:
- "42" rewards Douglas Adams readers (Hitchhiker's Guide)
- "Seldon" rewards Isaac Asimov readers (Foundation)
- "heat death" rewards anyone who has stared at cosmological entropy

These are not universal triggers. They are specific. Rarity by design.

The LEET HOUR (13:37) is the only fully hidden time badge.
It does not appear in any UI hint. It must be discovered.
