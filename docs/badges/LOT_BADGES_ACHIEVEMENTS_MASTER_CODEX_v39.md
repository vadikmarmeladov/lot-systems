## WORD TURN v29 — THE OPERATOR'S HANDBOOK

> *Every intelligence operation begins with a field report. Every self-care
> practice begins with an honest account of where you are. The journal is
> the field report. You are the operative. The mission is staying alive
> and well in the field of your own life.*

| ID                   | Symbol   | Trigger Word(s)                                         | Rarity   |
|----------------------|----------|---------------------------------------------------------|----------|
| `deep_cover`         | ▓·○·▓    | deep cover / undercover / going dark / off the grid     | uncommon |
| `field_report`       | ─·■·─    | field report / sitrep / situation report / status update | common  |
| `assets_secured`     | ◈·●·◈    | assets secured / locked down / protected my / secured it | uncommon|
| `blown_cover`        | ●·!·●    | blown / exposed / they know / cover blown / caught out  | rare     |
| `exfil_route`        | →·○·→    | exfil / extraction / escape route / way out / exit plan | rare     |
| `handler_brief`      | ○·■·○    | handler / briefing / mission briefing / debriefed       | uncommon |
| `need_to_know`       | ■·?·■    | need to know / classified / above my / restricted access | rare    |
| `dead_drop`          | ○·▓      | dead drop / left a note / passed it on / dropped it     | epic     |
| `clean_slate`        | □·○·□    | clean slate / starting fresh / wiped / new file / reset | rare     |
| `burn_notice`        | ●·∅·●    | burn notice / burned / cut loose / gone dark / terminated | epic   |
| `ghost_protocol`     | ·○·      | ghost protocol / shadow mode / no trace / vanished / ghost | epic  |
| `mission_complete`   | ■→●      | mission complete / objective reached / done and done / complete | rare|

**Detection**: All v29 words scanned via `detectWordTurns()` in WORD_TURNS array.

**Self-Care Resonance — The Operator's Handbook Map:**
- `deep_cover` → going off the grid is a self-care act when the grid is consuming you. The operative who knows when to go dark knows when silence is strategy. The journal names the choice.
- `field_report` → a sitrep is not a complaint. It is an honest account of what is, without judgment. The daily entry is the field report. The more accurate it is, the more useful it is.
- `assets_secured` → the operative protects resources. Your energy, attention, sleep, and relationships are assets. Write about securing them. The journal acknowledges what you guard.
- `blown_cover` → when the mask comes off — when the performance stops — that is not failure. That is the moment the actual work begins. Write about being seen.
- `exfil_route` → every good operator knows the exit before they enter. What is your plan for when you need to leave? Naming the exit is the beginning of freedom.
- `handler_brief` → the handler gives context, direction, and a reminder of the mission. A therapist, mentor, coach, or trusted friend plays this role. Write about your handler.
- `need_to_know` → not everything needs to be shared with everyone. The operator knows the difference between classified and secret. Write about what you are keeping to yourself, and why.
- `dead_drop` → the dead drop is information left for someone else to find. A journal entry is a dead drop for your future self. Write it like they will find it in six months and need to understand.
- `clean_slate` → the mission ends. The file is closed. The slate is wiped. Tomorrow is a new op. Write about what you are releasing, closing, completing.
- `burn_notice` → to be burned is to have your cover revoked, your identity erased by the organization. There is a self-care version: the relationship, job, or role that no longer recognizes you. Write about what has burned you, and what you have burned.
- `ghost_protocol` → going ghost is disappearing by design. Not running away — stepping back strategically. The self-care practitioner who knows how to go ghost knows how to rest without guilt.
- `mission_complete` → write it. Say it out loud on the page. The task is done. The objective is reached. The operative debriefs. The journal receives the completion.

## CALENDAR EASTER EGGS v27 — THE OPS CALENDAR

> *The birthdays and significant dates of the minds that defined the spy fiction vocabulary — the genre that made self-discipline and observation into art.*

| ID              | Symbol  | Date   | Significance                                                  | Rarity |
|-----------------|---------|--------|---------------------------------------------------------------|--------|
| `bond_day`      | ■·∅·■   | Jan 13 | Ian Fleming born 1908 — creator of James Bond 007            | rare   |
| `spy_wednesday` | ▓·■·▓   | Nov 5  | Guy Fawkes Night — the spy who nearly changed everything      | uncommon|
| `le_carre_day`  | ○·■·○   | Oct 19 | John le Carré born 1931 — the author who made espionage human | rare  |

**Lore:**
- `bond_day` — January 13, 1908. Ian Fleming was born in London. He served in Naval Intelligence during World War II and channeled everything he observed into the Bond novels. 007 is a fantasy of competence and control. Bond always has the plan. Bond always knows the exit. But the real op is the journal: the honest version where you don't always have the plan and sometimes you don't know the exit, and you write it down anyway.
- `spy_wednesday` — November 5, 1605. Guy Fawkes and the Gunpowder Plot. The plan was discovered. The cover was blown. Remember, remember: the self-care version is the recognition that plans are revealed as much as they are executed. Check in on Guy Fawkes Night and write about a plan that was discovered — by you, about yourself.
- `le_carre_day` — October 19, 1931. John le Carré (David Cornwell) was born in Poole, Dorset. He worked for MI5 and MI6 before becoming the greatest spy novelist of the 20th century. His spies are tired, compromised, and human. George Smiley is not glamorous. He is thorough. The self-care practitioner who reads le Carré recognizes the long game, the long haul, and the value of being underestimated.

**Lore:**
- `fleming_signal` — Ian Fleming wrote 14 James Bond novels between 1953 and 1966. - `eyes_only` — The most restricted classification: for your eyes only. The journal is the original "eyes only" document. No one else is cleared. No one else needs to be. Write those three words in your journal and the deepest lock in the system opens.
