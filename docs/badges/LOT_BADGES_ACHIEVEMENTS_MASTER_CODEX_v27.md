## THEMATIC NOTE — THE ARCADE PROTOCOL v17

The Arcade Protocol marks a deliberate return to the physical register of LOT's badge architecture.

**Previous late-engine themes** drew from:
- Literary philosophy (Quantum Library — entanglement, singularity)
- Astronomical mythology (Starship Deck — astronaut, telemetry)
- Alchemical transformation (Alchemist — transmute, crucible)

**v17 returns to**:
- Retro arcade vocabulary as *self-care grammar* — the genre that taught generations to try again
- Gaming moves as *somatic metaphors* — respawn, checkpoint, combo are body knowledge before they are words
- Three Secret Boss Easter eggs (Street Fighter, Zero Wing, World of Warcraft) as *three roots* of gaming culture's public language

The word "respawn" was born in online gaming (circa 1993–1995). The word "checkpoint" existed in racing and military before it entered games. The word "combo" came from fighting games and entered everyday speech as a unit of flow. These words earned their place in the protocol.

The Konami Code — ↑↑↓↓←→←→BA — has 10 inputs. Ten consecutive journal days.
The pattern is the practice.
