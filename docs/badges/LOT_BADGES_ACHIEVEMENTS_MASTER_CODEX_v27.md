## THEMATIC NOTE — THE NEON ARCADE v17

The Neon Arcade marks the entry of classic video game vocabulary into the LOT badge architecture as a dedicated engine.

Previous touches on gaming vocabulary:
- **v7 Rogue Archive** — dungeon-crawling RPG mechanics (loot, boss, respawn)
- **v9 Arcade Cabinet** — surface-level arcade tokens (coin, pixel, score)

**v17 goes deeper**: the vocabulary of game *mechanics* and *philosophy*.

The `checkpoint` is not a location — it is the system's promise that your progress is real.
The `continue screen` is not failure — it is the game's belief that you should keep going.
The `speedrun` is not impatience — it is the mastery of knowing every route.
The `combo` is not accumulation — it is the flow state made visible as number.
The `free play` is not absence of rules — it is the practice of play without objective, which is rest.

The three Secret Boss references follow the Neon Arcade's dual theme of gaming and computing:
- **Hideo Kojima** — game design as mythology, boss fights as philosophy
- **Alan Turing** — the theoretical architect of the machine you are using
- **The Konami Code** — the oldest easter egg, the first password that was also a joke, which became real

The Neon Arcade does not celebrate nostalgia. It recognizes that the mechanics of games are metaphors for real behavioral states: flow, resilience, rest, personal best, the return after absence.
