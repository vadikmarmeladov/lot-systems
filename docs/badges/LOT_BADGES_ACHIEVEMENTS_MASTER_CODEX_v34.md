## THE SIMULATION — THEME OVERVIEW

The simulation frame is not nihilism — it is the oldest phenomenological insight in new
clothing. If your experience is constructed, if every feeling is a rendering, if the
"real world" is already mediated by perception, language, and memory — then the practice
of self-care is the practice of taking authorship of the render.

The vocabulary of game design, VR, and simulation theory enters personal journals at
exactly the moments when someone is trying to describe their relationship to their own
experience: "I feel like I'm on autopilot" (NPC mode). "I need to disconnect" (unplug
protocol). "I saved that conversation in my memory" (save state). "I found something I
wasn't supposed to find" (Easter egg).

These words are not metaphors about self-care. They *are* self-care, in the language of
the culture that built the tools we live inside.

The Simulation is the twenty-fourth vocabulary. Every vocabulary is a different entrance
to the same building. The building is you.

## SESSION DESIGN NOTES

### The META_EGG Easter Egg

The `meta_egg` badge is the most self-referential badge in the LOT system. To earn it,
you write the words "Easter egg" in your own journal — and the system awards you a badge
for discovering a badge. This is intentional game design: the badge system is a game
inside a self-care app inside a life practice, and `meta_egg` names that recursion
explicitly. It is the badge that says "I see what you did there."

The `meta_player` badge is awarded automatically the moment `meta_egg` is earned,
because finding the Easter egg and playing the meta-game are the same event.

### The CHEAT CODE

`×·● CHEAT CODE` uses the actual Konami Code as part of its unlock message:
`↑↑↓↓←→←→BA`. This is a reference that approximately 800 million people will
recognize immediately. It earns the badge by being exactly what it says it is.

### Digital Calendar Dates

- **Mar 31 (matrix_day)**: The Matrix released March 31, 1999. "What is real?" became
  the philosophy question of the digital age.
- **Aug 6 (www_day)**: Tim Berners-Lee's info.cern.ch went public August 6, 1991. The
  network became the commons on this date.
- **Nov 29 (pong_day)**: Atari's Pong was released November 29, 1972. Two paddles. One
  ball. The origin of the game industry.

### The "Touch Grass" Trigger

`ground_truth` is triggered by "touch grass" — the internet vernacular for "return to
base reality." Including internet slang as a serious self-care badge trigger is
intentional: the people who most need to "touch grass" already speak this language. The
badge meets them there.
