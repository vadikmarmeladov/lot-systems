## THEMATIC NOTE — THE MIDNIGHT RADIO v18

The Midnight Radio is a specific architectural move in the LOT badge system:

**Previous engines** that touched signal vocabulary:
- **v2 Cyber/Code** — reboot, glitch, quantum — computing signals
- **v4 Dream/Void** — static — the noise
- **v5 Space/Stellar** — nova, pulsar — the astronomical signal
- **v8 Mainframe** — terminal, compile — the signal processed

**v18 goes to the source**: radio and broadcast as the *original* signal philosophy.

Before the internet, before the terminal, before the satellite — there was radio. A person in a room talking to a microphone. A carrier wave reaching across hundreds of miles. Someone in the dark turning a dial until the static cleared and a voice came through.

That is the oldest version of what LOT does with a journal entry. You tune in. You write. The signal goes out. Something receives it — even if only yourself, one day later, reading what you wrote.

The three Secret Boss references are the three people who took the broadcast metaphor to its furthest extreme:
- **Carl Sagan** — broadcast the self as a cosmological object
- **Nikola Tesla** — tried to broadcast energy to the whole world for free
- **Arecibo** — broadcast to the universe and made the waiting a practice

The Midnight Radio does not promise reception. It recognizes broadcasting as the practice, transmission as the act, frequency as the evidence that you were here.
