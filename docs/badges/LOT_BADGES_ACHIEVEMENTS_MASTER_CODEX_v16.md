## PART XI — DESIGN DOCTRINE

### The Five Laws of LOT Badges

1. **Badges are discovered, not earned by menu.** The system watches. The player expresses. The badge fires.

2. **Hidden badges reward authentic expression.** No one should write "journey" to get `journey_mode`. They write it because that's how they think of their life. The badge confirms what was already true.

3. **Rarity is earned, not purchased.** A `mythic` badge means you have done something exceptional over months or years. There is no shortcut.

4. **Every badge has a story.** `towel_day` is not just "check in on May 25." It is a tribute to Douglas Adams, to absurdist wisdom, to the idea that preparation is love. The badge is a small monument.

5. **The system remembers everything.** Badges are permanent. They accumulate. At 284 types in v16, the badge system is itself a biography — a record of what the user expressed, when they showed up, and how long they stayed.

### Word Turn Philosophy

Word turns operate on the principle that **language is self-disclosure**. When someone writes about their "journey" or "chapter," they are using narrative frames unconsciously. The badge system makes that unconscious frame visible and acknowledged.

The Book Lexicon (v7) extends this to literary structure: hero, villain, quest, ending, author. These are archetypes. When users write them, they are casting their lives as narrative. The system honors that cast.

### Time Easter Egg Philosophy

Time easter eggs operate on **attentiveness**. Only someone who checks in at 00:42 with intentionality — or who happens to be awake at that precise threshold — receives `the_answer`. The time gates reward presence in the moment.

The Deep Night Codes (v7) are particularly significant:
- `the_answer` at 00:42 — for the late-night thinkers who seek meaning
- `devils_hour` at 03:33 — for those between sleep and waking
- `infinity_gate` at 08:08 — for the morning practitioners who see loops
