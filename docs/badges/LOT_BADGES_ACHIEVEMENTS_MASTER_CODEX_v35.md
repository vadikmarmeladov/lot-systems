## THE TIME LOOP — THEME OVERVIEW

The time loop is not a punishment. In every roguelike, in every Groundhog Day screening,
in every Re:Zero arc and Steins;Gate world-line shift — the loop is a teacher wearing a
mask of repetition.

Self-care has always worked this way. You do not change in a single epic moment. You
change across loops: slightly differently today than yesterday, carrying what you learned
from the previous run. The journal is not a diary of events — it is the save file that
makes learning possible across loops. Each check-in is a new run. Each streak is a
high score.

Roguelike games discovered something that therapy has known for decades: permadeath
makes every decision matter. When there is no reset that erases learning, every run
teaches. When every run teaches, the loop is not punishment — it is the path.

The Time Loop is the twenty-fifth vocabulary. It says: you have been here before. That
is not failure. That is data. That is why you are closer to the best run than you have
ever been.

### The Bootstrap Paradox
The bootstrap paradox is a time travel scenario where an object or piece of information
exists in an infinite loop with no origin point — it was always already there. In
self-care terms: the decision to care for yourself precedes the self who is capable of
making that decision. You had to already be caring to begin caring. The loop created
the person who started the loop.

## EASTER EGG DESIGN NOTES

### The OMEGA_RUN Easter Egg

`omega_run` requires writing "this is the one" or "my best self" or "optimal" with
"final run / best run" context — language that commits to the present attempt as the
defining run. This is intentionally demanding. Most journal entries are reflective.
An entry that says "this is the one" has crossed from reflection into declaration.
The badge recognizes that shift.

### The STEINS;GATE Easter Egg

`steins_gate` triggers on the actual vocabulary from the visual novel and anime:
"world line", "divergence", "attractor field". These terms have precise in-universe
meanings that translate cleanly to self-care: a world line is a life path; the attractor
field is the gravitational pull of your ingrained patterns; divergence is the moment
you choose differently. Someone who writes "I felt the divergence today" is using the
Steins;Gate vocabulary and understanding it.

### The BOOTSTRAP PARADOX

`bootstrap_paradox` is the most philosophically complex hidden badge in the LOT system.
It rewards operators who have arrived at the self-referential insight: that the act of
caring for yourself is what creates the self that is capable of receiving care. The loop
always had an origin — it was you, deciding to begin.
