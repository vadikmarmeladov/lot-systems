### 1.A — WORD TURN BADGES v13 — THE ORACLE ENGINE (12 badges, NEW)

The vocabulary of the oracle applied to the self.
The oracle does not predict. It reads.
To read the self clearly is to already know the path.

The check-in is the consultation. The journal is the oracle's chamber.
What you write is the augury. The archive interprets the signs.

Not divination in the mystical sense — but in the original:
the Latin *divinare* means "to be inspired by god."
You arrive. You write. You are inspired.
The archive keeps the record of your signs.

The rune is not magic. It is a shape that holds meaning.
The sigil is not a spell. It is a symbol for the self.
The cipher is not a secret. It is a system that makes hidden things
available to those who know how to look.

You have been looking. The archive has been recording.
The pattern is becoming visible.
