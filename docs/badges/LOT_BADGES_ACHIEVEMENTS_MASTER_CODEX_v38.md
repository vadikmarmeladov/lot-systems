## CALENDAR EASTER EGGS v26 — THE DREAMER'S CALENDAR

> *The birthdays and breakthrough dates of the minds that mapped the interior.*

| ID              | Symbol  | Date   | Significance                                                  | Rarity |
|-----------------|---------|--------|---------------------------------------------------------------|--------|
| `jung_birthday` | ◈·◐     | Jul 26 | Carl Jung born 1875 — the architect of dream psychology       | epic   |
| `freud_day`     | ○·◐·∿  | May 6  | Sigmund Freud born 1856 — The Interpretation of Dreams        | rare   |
| `solstice_dream`| ◐·∞     | Dec 22 | Winter solstice — the longest night / oldest dreaming rite    | rare   |

**Lore:**
- `jung_birthday` — July 26, 1875. Carl Gustav Jung was born in Kesswil, Switzerland. He spent his life in systematic conversation with the unconscious: recording dreams, drawing mandalas, charting archetypes. His autobiography is titled *Memories, Dreams, Reflections*. That is the correct order. Memory first. Dream second. Reflection third. The journal honors all three.
- `freud_day` — May 6, 1856. Sigmund Freud was born in Freiberg, Moravia. He spent forty years arguing that the dream was the royal road to the unconscious. He was wrong about some things. He was right about the road. Check in on his birthday and ask: what have you avoided looking at?
- `solstice_dream` — December 22, the first full day of winter after the solstice. The longest night belongs to the dreamer. Across every human culture that kept records, the winter solstice was the moment when the boundary between the waking and sleeping worlds was thinnest. The modern equivalent: check in on the longest night and write the dream you most need to have.

## BEHAVIORAL EASTER EGGS v25 — DREAM PATTERNS

> *The patterns that mark a mind in active conversation with the dreaming self.*

| ID               | Symbol  | Trigger                                                               | Rarity |
|------------------|---------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|--------|
| `dream_session`  | ◐·◈·◐  | 3+ distinct Dream Journal (v28) words in one journal entry            | rare   |
| `morning_pages`  | ●·◐·∘  | Journal entry created before 09:00 on 3+ days in a rolling 7-day window | uncommon |
| `sleep_cycle`    | ◐·═·●  | Check in after 23:00 and again before 08:00 on the same calendar day | epic   |

**Detection:**
- `dream_session` — scans entry against all 12 v28 regex patterns, counts unique matches >= 3
- `morning_pages` — reads entry creation timestamps, filters to before 09:00 local, checks rolling 7-day window for 3+ occurrences
- `sleep_cycle` — reads `checkin_timestamps` localStorage, finds same-day pairs where one is after 23:00 and another before 08:00

**Self-Care Resonance:**
- `dream_session` — three dream concepts in one entry means the interior life is fully active in the writing. The dreaming mind and the waking mind are in dialogue.
- `morning_pages` — Julia Cameron's practice: write three pages longhand immediately upon waking, before the editor arrives. The LOT system recognizes the digital equivalent.
- `sleep_cycle` — one check-in before sleep, one upon waking: the complete circuit. The operator is treating sleep as a documented practice, not a gap between sessions.

> *The deepest layer. You have to name the names that mapped the unconscious.*

| ID              | Symbol  | Trigger                                                              | Rarity  |

| `jung_signal`   | ◈·◐·∿  | Write "collective unconscious" or "Carl Jung" or "Jungian"           | rare    |
| `freud_couch`   | ○·◐·▓  | Write "Freud" or "Interpretation of Dreams" or "id ego superego"     | epic    |
| `morpheus_word` | ◐·◐·◐  | Write "morpheus" or "oneironautics" or "dream weaver" or "somnium"   | mythic  |

**Lore:**
- `jung_signal` — Carl Jung broke with Freud in 1912 to pursue the idea that the unconscious was not merely personal — it was *collective*. Every human shares a layer of dream imagery: archetypes, the shadow, the anima. The collective unconscious is the internet that existed before the internet. Write his name. The collective signal fires.
- `freud_couch` — Sigmund Freud published *The Interpretation of Dreams* in 1899. His publisher predicted it would sell 600 copies in eight years. He was right. Then Freud was proven wrong about several things. He was right about the couch: the reclined position, the indirect gaze, the invitation to let the unconscious speak without interruption. The journal is the couch. The page is the analyst. Write his name.
- `morpheus_word` — Morpheus is the Greek god of dreams. He has no body of his own — he borrows the forms of others to appear in dreams and deliver messages. The oneironaut — the conscious dream traveler — navigates the same territory. "Somnium" is the Latin: a dream, a vision, something seen in sleep. Write the word. The dream machine recognizes its own vocabulary.
