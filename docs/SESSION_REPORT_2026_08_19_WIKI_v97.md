## 4. SYSTEM OBSERVATION

The QIoT™ ecosystem bridge in FM v123 is the first background job to scan
the physical environment layer. J58 does not detect behavioral patterns —
it detects the ecosystem configuration: which nodes are active, whether the
physical loop is closed, whether all 6 nodes are coherent.

This is architecturally significant. J1–J57 scan the operator's behavioral
and cognitive signal. J58 scans the environment around the operator.
The system now asks: not just "what are you doing?" but "where are you doing it?"

The distinction between FULL_COHERENCE and ROBOT_ACTIVE and PARTIAL is
a state taxonomy for the physical environment:
```
FULL_COHERENCE  — all 6 nodes active, ecosystem signal synchronized
ROBOT_ACTIVE    — ROBOT node specifically contributing (home automation live)
PARTIAL         — some nodes active, incomplete physical loop
```

The COCKPIT-RULE compression pass in FM v123 is doctrine refinement, not
feature addition. The handlers are not smarter — they are cleaner. Every prose
row replaced with a gauge reading is an act of military purity. The cockpit
does not narrate. It reports.

Badge v33 THE DUNGEON MASTER completes the D&D vocabulary trilogy:
v26 THE DUNGEON CRAWLER (the player vocabulary), v33 THE DUNGEON MASTER
(the game-master vocabulary). The distinction matters:
The Dungeon Crawler uses the dungeon. The Dungeon Master builds it.
In self-care terms: the operator graduates from navigating the interior world
to understanding its architecture. COSMIC badge: dungeon_master.
