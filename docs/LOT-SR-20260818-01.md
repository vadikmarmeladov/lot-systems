## BUILD — v33 (THE DUNGEON MASTER)

### Design Philosophy

Tabletop RPG (D&D, 1974) as self-care vocabulary. Every journal entry is a
dungeon turn. Every check-in is rolling for initiative. Every streak is a
level-up. The dungeon is the mind. The dragon is avoidance. The party is
community. The paladin oath is the commitment you renew. The tavern is rest.

This engine completes the 20th Word Turn vocabulary set.
