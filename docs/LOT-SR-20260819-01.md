## DESIGN NOTES — THE TIME MACHINE

### Why temporal vocabulary?

After v26's dungeon/RPG vocabulary (boss fight, loot, level up, rest point), the
natural next vector was time. The Dungeon Crawler treats the self-care journal as
a dungeon to navigate. The Time Machine treats it as a temporal record — a machine
for moving through past and future selves.

Every self-care journal is fundamentally a time travel device. You write in the
present, you read in the future, you reference the past. The vocabulary of time
travel — timeline, epoch, turning point, flux state, paradox — is native to
serious personal development practice.

### Easter egg strategy

The three calendar dates (Aug 15, Sep 21, Oct 26) were chosen to span the
calendar without clustering. Aug 15 and Sep 21 are obscure enough to be
genuine surprises; Oct 26 (*Back to the Future*) will be recognized by most
users but is still rare enough to feel special.

The three hidden Secret Boss badges (marty_mcfly, wells_key, time_loop_omega)
escalate from playful (McFly references) to literary (Wells) to therapeutic
(naming your time loop). The MYTHIC `time_loop_omega` is the deepest of the
three — recognizing the clinical reality that naming a behavioral loop is the
first step to interrupting it.

### Self-care integration

```
anchor_point  (COMMON)  — the most accessible v27 badge; "anchor" is
                          already in therapeutic vocabulary. High detection
                          frequency expected.

past_self + future_self — the twin temporal-self arc. These two together
                          (or with timeline_scan) form the full_loop
                          behavioral badge. Designed to reward the kind
                          of temporal self-compassion work that good
                          journaling naturally produces.

flux_state    (RARE)    — acknowledges the discomfort of being mid-change.
                          Naming "flux" in a journal is already a therapeutic
                          act. The badge rewards the naming.

time_witnessed (LEGENDARY) — the highest-rarity word turn badge, requiring
                          "timeless" / "eternal" — language that appears only
                          in deep practice, after long engagement.
```
