# COSMO® Cube — User Manual

**A quiet notification companion for your LOT® profile.**

---

## What's in the box

- 1x COSMO® Cube (stainless steel, two-part shell)
- 1x Charging Plate (40mm x 40mm silver plate)
- 1x USB-C charging cable
- This manual

---

## Meet your Cube

The Cube has two faces:

- **The mirror face** — polished stainless steel. This is the resting
  face. When your Cube isn't telling you anything, leave this side up.
- **The active face** — a small round screen, a camera, and a single
  button labeled **COPY**. Turn the Cube to this side when you want to
  read a notification or use the button.

There is no power switch, no speaker, and no app to install on the Cube
itself. Everything happens through your existing LOT® account at
lot-systems.com.

---

## Setting up your Cube

1. Place the Cube on the Charging Plate. The plate's edge light will
   pulse gently while it charges.
2. On your phone or computer, open **lot-systems.com/hardware**.
3. A pairing code will appear on screen. Turn your Cube to the active
   face and hold it toward the code — the camera reads it in about a
   second.
4. Once paired, the screen shows a short confirmation, then goes quiet.
   Your Cube is now yours.

A Cube only ever belongs to one LOT profile at a time. You can unpair it
at any time from lot-systems.com/hardware — this is instant and cannot
be undone remotely, so re-pairing requires the physical Cube again.

---

## What your Cube does

Your Cube receives short messages from your LOT® Memory Engine — the
same system that already asks you thoughtful questions in the app. When
it has something worth telling you, the screen lights up with a short
message, for example:

> Coffee time!

That's it. No feed, no scrolling, no unread badge piling up. If you miss
it, it will still be there — reflected in your Log — but the Cube itself
does not nag.

---

## The COPY button

Press **COPY** at any time. Whatever the Cube last showed you is saved
straight into your **Log** on lot-systems.com — the same place your
journal entries live. Think of it as a physical bookmark: a message that
mattered enough to press a button for is a message worth keeping in your
own record.

If the screen is quiet when you press COPY, your Cube simply notes that
you were present — a small, honest "I was here" entry.

---

## Charging

Set the Cube on its plate whenever you're not using it — there is no
harm in leaving it there overnight. A full charge from empty takes a
few hours. The plate's edge light tells you what's happening:

- **Slow pulse** — charging
- **Steady** — fully charged
- **Off** — not connected to power, or no Cube present

---

## The weather sensor

Your Cube quietly reads the temperature, humidity, and air pressure of
the room it sits in. This shows up on your LOT profile the same way
weather already does today, just measured where you actually are instead
of the nearest weather station.

---

## Care and cleaning

- The mirror face is polished stainless steel — a soft microfiber cloth
  keeps it bright. Avoid abrasive cleaners.
- The active face is matte-finished on purpose — do not polish it.
- The Cube is splash-resistant, not waterproof. Keep it away from
  standing water.

---

## Privacy, in plain terms

- The camera only ever turns on during pairing. It is not a security
  camera and does not record or send video.
- The microphone listens only for a wake cue on the device itself — raw
  audio never leaves the Cube.
- Everything the Cube reports becomes part of your own Memory Story,
  under your own account, the same privacy rules that already govern
  the rest of your LOT profile (README, "Your Story, Your Data") — you
  can export or delete it at any time.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Try this |
|---|---|
| Screen never lights up | Confirm the Cube is charged and paired at lot-systems.com/hardware |
| COPY button doesn't seem to save anything | Check your Log tab directly — entries appear there, not on the Cube |
| Plate light doesn't turn on | Check the USB-C cable and power source |
| Need to give the Cube to someone else | Unpair it first at lot-systems.com/hardware, then pair it again on their account |
