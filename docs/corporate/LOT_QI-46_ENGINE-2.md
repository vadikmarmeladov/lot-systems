## III. HONEST BOUNDARY (read this before the rest)

The brief that produced this node used language like *"extract the engine
that is based on people's soul and emotions"* and *"upload a person's being."*
That is the LOT® poetic register — the same register v0.2 uses when it calls
inference COGS "Heat: Emotional signal" or calls the founding cohort the
"Soul Disk." It is brand voice, not a technical spec.

What QI·46 actually is, stated plainly:

- A fine-tuned inference layer over LOT®'s own product corpus (journals,
  session logs, white papers, brand copy) plus a subscriber's own logged,
  consented inputs (the Calibration Loop).
- It does not, and under this doctrine will never, claim to copy, extract,
  or store a person's consciousness, "soul," or emotional interior as such.
  It models *declared and logged behavior* — text a person wrote, a rating
  they gave, a pattern in when they show up — and generates a response
  calibrated to that history.
- "Humanoid output" (grace, poetry, warmth, presence — the brief's "hugs,"
  "being there," "being cool") is a description of RESPONSE GRAMMAR (Layer 3
  in v0.2): tone constraints on generated text. It is not a claim about
  embodiment, a physical humanoid, or gender identity assigned to the engine.
  Per v0.2 Layer 3, QI·46 is never described to a subscriber in anthropomorphic
  terms unless the subscriber initiates that framing.

Any future session that reads "soul," "being," or "upload" in a LOT® brief
should resolve it against this section, not re-litigate it.
