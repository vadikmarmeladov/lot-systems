# COSMO® Cube
## User Manual — v1.0

LOT®† COSMO® — a hardware companion for lot-systems.com

---

## Welcome

COSMO® Cube is a small stainless-steel computer that sits on your desk.
It shows you one short message at a time — a nudge from your LOT®
Systems Memory Engine, like "Coffee time!" — and it has one button:
**COPY**. Press it, and a note appears instantly in your Log tab on
lot-systems.com.

That's the whole device. No app to check, no feed to scroll. A pager for
a life you're already building on LOT®.

## The two faces

**Polished face (back):** mirror-finished stainless steel. This is the
side that rests on the charging puck.

**Front face:** satin-finished stainless steel, with three openings —

| Element | Location | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Camera | top-left | presence check + operator-triggered snapshot |
| Screen | center | shows your current message |
| COPY button | bottom edge | logs a note to your Log tab |

---

## Setting it up

1. **Charge it.** Set the Cube face-down (polished side down) on the
   charging puck. The screen will show `Charging...` briefly.
2. **Pair it.** On your phone, open lot-systems.com/settings and tap
   **Pair COSMO Cube**. Scan the code shown on your phone with the
   Cube's camera, or hold your phone near the Cube (Bluetooth) and
   follow the prompts.
3. **Choose your Wi-Fi.** Enter your home Wi-Fi password once, on your
   phone — the Cube never needs a keyboard.
4. **Done.** The screen will show `Paired.` and then settle into normal
   operation — blank until your first notification arrives.

---

## Using it

**Receiving a message.** The Cube checks in with lot-systems.com quietly
in the background. When your Memory Engine has something for you — a
question ready, a badge unlocked, a weather shift, a simple nudge like
"Coffee time!" — it appears on the screen and stays there until the next
one. Nothing blinks, nothing chimes. It's there when you look.

**Pressing COPY.** Press the button any time. The screen briefly shows
`Sent.` and a new entry appears in your Log tab on lot-systems.com,
timestamped, with your battery level and the message that was on screen.
Use it to mark a moment — "I saw this, noted" — without touching your
phone.

**Weather.** The Cube quietly reports the temperature, humidity, and
pressure around it back to your profile. No setup needed.

---

## Charging

Set the Cube face-down on the puck any time. A full charge from empty
takes about 90 minutes. On typical use, one charge lasts several days —
the screen only draws power when a new message arrives, not while
holding one.

---

## Care

- Wipe the polished face with a soft cloth; avoid abrasive cleaners.
- Not submersible. Keep away from standing water (dust- and
  splash-resistant only).
- If the Cube stops responding, hold the COPY button for 10 seconds to
  factory-reset and re-pair from step 2 above.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to try |
|---|---|
| Screen is blank and stays blank | Check the charging puck is powered; place the Cube back on it for 10 minutes |
| `Pairing failed` on screen | Confirm your phone's Bluetooth is on and you're within 1 meter during pairing |
| COPY press doesn't appear in Log tab | Check your home Wi-Fi is online; the Cube will retry automatically once reconnected |
| Message never changes | This is normal if the Memory Engine has nothing new — the screen holds the last message rather than going dark |
