# LOT® Usership Systems — FMCG Subscription Architecture (Plan 2027)

## Business Plan: The Physical Essential Pivot

---

## Executive Summary

This strategic expansion transitions LOT® from a pure digital network into a
physical-first utility provider. By bundling critical everyday essentials —
ranging from hygiene paper goods to premium apparel basics — into a
high-retention annual membership, we solve the cash-flow challenges typical of
traditional e-commerce.

The core physical offering is the Basic Essentials CPG/FMCG subscription priced
at $399/month (billed as $4,788/year upfront). This tier establishes a persistent
physical touchpoint in the user's home. It provides automated, monthly systematic
refills of baseline necessities like toothbrushes, high-quality underwear,
packaged goods, and personal hygiene paper products.

Growth is accelerated by modular, highly profitable $99/month upgrades tailored
to the evolving structural needs of the user's environment (Basics, Kids, and
Home).

manufacturing and bulk logistics.

**Net Operating Leverage:** Fixed IT/AI infrastructure cost: $500/month
($6,000/year). Gross cash surplus prior to fulfillment: **$148,800**.
