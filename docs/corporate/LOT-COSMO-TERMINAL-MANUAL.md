# COSMO® Terminal — Operator Manual

**v1.0 Pilot Unit — 100-Unit Run**
**LOT Systems Corporation**

---

## What this is

COSMO® Terminal is a small stainless-steel device that sits on your desk,
charges wirelessly, and shows you one short message at a time from
lot-systems.com — the way a pager once did, without a feed, without a
badge count, without a screen that's ever lit unless there's something to
say.

It has one button: **Copy**. Pressing it saves whatever the device is
showing into your Log on lot-systems.com, so a passing notification
becomes something you can find again later.

---

## In the box

- 1× COSMO® Terminal (stainless steel, two-part body)
- 1× Wireless charging pad
- 1× Quick-start card

---

## Getting to know the device

COSMO® Terminal has two faces:

- **The polished face** — mirror-finish stainless steel, no markings.
  This is the resting face. When you're not expecting a notification,
  this side faces up.
- **The instrument face** — matte stainless steel. This side carries the
  screen, the camera, and the Copy button. When a notification is
  expected, this side faces up.

You can tell which face is which by touch alone — polished vs. matte —
without needing to look.

---

## Setup

1. **Place the device on the charging pad.** Either face down works for
   charging; the coil is centered in the base.
2. **Wait for first boot.** The screen will display a 6-character pairing
   code.
3. **On lot-systems.com, go to Settings → Connect COSMO® Terminal**, and
   enter the code shown on the device.
   - COSMO® Terminal requires an eligible LOT Usership profile
     (Yellow-tier Benchmark or above) to pair. If pairing is refused,
     your account may not yet meet this threshold — this is by design,
     not an error. See docs/corporate/LOT_ROBOTICS_COSMO.md for the
     eligibility framework.
4. **Pairing complete.** The screen goes blank. This is normal — the
   screen is only ever lit when there's a message.

## Using COSMO® Terminal

### Receiving a notification

When lot-systems.com has something worth telling you — a self-care
nudge, a weather-linked reminder, a milestone — the screen lights with
one short line, for example:

> Coffee time.

The message stays on screen for 8 seconds, then the screen goes dark
again.

COSMO® Terminal will show you at most one message every 15 minutes, and
no more than 12 in a day. If it seems quiet, that's the device working
correctly — it is built to say less, not more.

### The Copy button

- **Press Copy while a message is showing** — that exact message is
  saved to your Log on lot-systems.com, timestamped to the moment you
  pressed it.
- **Press Copy while the screen is blank** — the device saves a manual
  check-in to your Log, using its own sensors (temperature, light) at
  that moment.
- A quick screen flash confirms every press. No sound.

### The weather sensor

COSMO® Terminal reads temperature, humidity, and pressure from wherever
it sits and quietly feeds that into your weather-linked notifications on
lot-systems.com — you don't need to do anything for this to work.

### The camera

The camera takes a single still frame only when you press Copy with a
blank screen, or when a notification specifically calls for it. It never
records video, never streams, and the image itself is never stored or
sent anywhere — only what the device concludes from it (for example,
"desk, dim, alone") is ever used. There is no way to view a saved photo
from COSMO® Terminal, because none is ever saved.

---

## Charging

Set the device on its wireless charging pad. A full charge from empty
takes several hours; COSMO® Terminal is designed to live on the pad
between uses rather than be carried, and a full charge should comfortably
last 72 hours or more of normal notification use.

---

## Care

- Wipe the polished face with a soft, dry cloth. Fingerprints on a mirror
  finish are normal — this is not a defect.
- Do not submerge. COSMO® Terminal is not rated for water exposure.
- The matte instrument face can be cleaned the same way; avoid abrasive
  cleaners near the screen and camera window.

---

## Unpairing

From lot-systems.com Settings, select "Disconnect COSMO® Terminal." The
device will return to pairing mode (a new code on screen) within a few
seconds. Unpairing does not erase your Log history — only future entries
stop.

---

## This is a pilot unit

You are one of 100 operators with this device. It is a v1.0 pilot run,
not a finished retail product — its firmware will update automatically
(only while on the charging pad, never mid-notification), and its
behavior may be refined based on what this pilot run learns. If something
seems wrong, that observation matters — this is exactly the phase where
it's useful to notice.
