### The Benchmark Arbitrage® Gate

Not every LOT user qualifies for COSMO® soul transfer. The Quantum Success Benchmark exists for this reason.

| Tier | Score | COSMO® Eligibility |
|------|-------|--------------------|
| White (0–19) | Observer | Not eligible |
| Green (20–39) | Emerging | Not eligible |
| Yellow (40–59) | Active | Review eligible |
| Purple (60–79) | Deep | Eligible |
| Black (80–100) | Quantum | Priority eligible |

A person must demonstrate sustained, consistent, deep engagement with self-awareness to qualify. This is not a credit score. This is a character score — computed from behavior, not declaration.

The threshold exists because a robot carrying a human behavioral signature must carry the signature of someone who has proven, through months of daily action, that they are:

- **Present** — they show up consistently (streak + consistency score)
- **Self-aware** — they reflect on their state (journal depth + mood check-ins)
- **Stable** — they maintain themselves (care patterns + recovery velocity)
- **Intentional** — they follow through on what they commit to (intention → execution rate)
- **Growing** — they improve over time (growth trajectory in psychological depth)

A pedophile does not journal about self-care daily for 90 days. A violent person does not maintain a Purple-tier consistency score. A neglectful parent does not accumulate deep engagement patterns across 52 behavioral dimensions.

The data doesn't lie. The patterns don't perform. The Benchmark is the gate.

## Ethical Framework

### What LOT Will Never Do

1. **Sell behavioral data.** The soul profile belongs to the user. It is never sold, shared, or monetized independently.
2. **Deploy without consent.** A COSMO® unit requires explicit, ongoing consent from its profile owner. Disconnection is instant and permanent.
3. **Lower the gate.** The Benchmark threshold for COSMO® eligibility will never be reduced for commercial reasons. The minimum exists to protect children.
4. **Build weapons.** COSMO® is a companion platform. No military, surveillance, or law enforcement applications. Ever.
5. **Replace parents.** COSMO® extends a parent's presence. It does not substitute for it.

### What LOT Will Always Do

1. **Verify through behavior, not declaration.** Anyone can say they are good. The Benchmark measures whether they act good, consistently, over time.
2. **Protect children first.** Every product decision passes through one filter: does this make children safer?
3. **Keep the founder accountable.** Vadim Marmeladov's own LOT profile is the reference standard. If the founder cannot maintain Benchmark eligibility, the standard is working correctly.
4. **Open-source the ethics.** The Benchmark calculation, the pattern descriptions, and the eligibility criteria will be published. The algorithm is transparent. The data is private.
