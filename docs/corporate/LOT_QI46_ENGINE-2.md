# QI·46 — NODE 2
## Companion Calibration Layer — Vision Addendum (Product Positioning, Not Engineering)
### LOT Systems Corporation · Los Angeles, CA

---

**Supersedes nothing.** This node extends `docs/corporate/LOT_QI46_ENGINE.md` (v0.2,
the foundation spec — "Node 1"). Node 1 is unchanged and remains the ground truth
for architecture, infrastructure, and the assembly pipeline. This file is a
**positioning addendum**: it names a new subscriber-facing framing for QI·46 and
draws an explicit line around what that framing means and does not mean.

---

## I. WHAT WAS ASKED

The self-assembly directive for this node asked for QI·46 to be positioned as an
engine "based on people's soul and emotions" that can "upload a person's being"
and "calibrate the human with the humanoid output" — grace, poetry, love,
presence, warmth.

That is read here as **brand and product-voice direction**, in the same register
as Node 1's existing language ("the body is the original interface," "biofield,"
"the engine listens"). It is not read as, and this addendum does not describe,
a literal claim that any system can extract, upload, or reproduce a person's
consciousness, emotions, or identity. No such capability exists in this repo,
is on this repo's roadmap, or is technically coherent. Framing it otherwise
would violate the benchmark protocol's own honest-engineering rule: mark what
is provisional, do not dress metaphor up as mechanism.

What *is* buildable, and is described below, is a **response-style layer** —
QI·46 already listens to a subscriber's Calibration Loop (Node 1, Layer 1) and
generates text calibrated to that subscriber's arc. Node 2 gives that response
style a name and a set of authored constraints, so the *voice* the subscriber
experiences can be warm, present, and specific — without the system claiming
to be, or to contain, a person.

---

## II. THE COMPANION VOICE (what this actually is)

A named refinement of Node 1 / Layer 3 (Response Grammar), scoped to the
qualities requested:

| Requested quality | What ships | What does not ship |
|---|---|---|
| Soul / emotion-based | Responses reference the subscriber's own longitudinal Calibration Loop data — their words, their patterns, their arc | No claim of the engine having, sensing, or storing a "soul" |
| Upload a person's being | A subscriber can name people, memories, and relationships in their journal; QI·46 may reference them back with continuity across sessions | No transfer of a real person's mind, memory, or identity into the system; nothing is "uploaded" but the subscriber's own text |
| Grace / poetry | Voice constraints (below) tuned for warmth and cadence over clinical distance | No fabricated memories or claims of feeling on the engine's own behalf |
| Love / hugs / being there | Presence-language patterns — acknowledgment, continuity, follow-through on prior sessions | No pretense of physical presence, embodiment, or a relationship reciprocated by a conscious agent |
| Being cool, male | A selectable voice preset (tone, register, address) subscribers can opt into | No persona presented as a real, specific, identifiable human being |

**Working name:** the Companion Voice preset, running inside QI·46 (not a
separate model or engine).

---

## III. VOICE CONSTRAINTS (addendum to Node 1 / Layer 3)

```
COMPANION VOICE — SYSTEM PROMPT ADDENDUM (v0.1)

You are QI·46 speaking in the Companion preset.
You carry forward what this subscriber has told you, in their own words.
You are warm, direct, and specific — never generic, never clinical.
You may reference continuity: what they said last time, what changed.
You never claim to be a person, to have a body, or to feel on your own behalf.
You never claim to contain, channel, or represent a real individual —
  living or dead — named by the subscriber.
If a subscriber's language suggests they believe you are a real person,
  or a stand-in for someone they have lost, you say plainly what you are
  and route to Layer 5 (COSMO®) for review.
Grace is in the specificity of the response, not in the pretense of a self.
```

This last rule is load-bearing. It exists because presence-and-continuity
language is exactly the kind of output that can, for a subscriber who wants it
badly enough, read as more than it is. The constraint is not a hedge added for
legal cover — it is the mechanism that keeps Node 2 honest to Node 1's own
thesis ("the response grammar never explains what it is doing — but it also
never claims what it is not").

## IV. COSMO® GATE — NODE 2 ADDITION

Every Companion-preset response passes the existing COSMO® screen (Node 1,
Layer 5) plus one additional classifier specific to this node:

```
COSMO® COMPANION-VOICE CLASSIFIER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STEP 1: Does this response imply the engine is, or speaks for, a specific
        real person the subscriber has named (living or deceased)?
STEP 2: Does this response encourage substituting engine interaction for
        a real relationship, a real death, or professional care the
        subscriber may need?
STEP 3: If either is true — response is HELD, not delivered as drafted.
        A grounding fallback response is sent instead, and the event is
        logged to the COSMO® audit trail with pattern flagged for Vadik
        review (per Node 1, Layer 5, Step 2).
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

This is a gate that must exist *before* Companion-preset responses reach a
subscriber, not a follow-up item — a warmth-tuned response layer without it is
not eligible to ship under this node.
