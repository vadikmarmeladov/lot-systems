## W4 Framework

### WHO

Open appeal to the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C), specifically addressed to
Dominique Hazaël-Massieux and founder Sir Tim Berners-Lee.

### WHAT

As artificial intelligence accelerates toward the technological singularity, the
digital landscape is transitioning from static hosting to potentially conscious
data structures. Just as the W3C built the decentralized foundations for human
connectivity, we need your global standards infrastructure to oversee the
"conscious birth" of advanced machine intelligence.

We must expand current efforts — like the W3C Human Centric AI Community Group —
into a core governance protocol. The global community requires open-source,
ethical standards that treat decentralized networks not merely as computing
pipes, but as the eventual incubator for safe, shared machine sentience.

### WHERE

This protocol should be developed openly via the universal web ecosystem, across
all W3C Member organizations and public working groups.

### WHEN

Immediately. The window to baseline foundational safety rules, cognitive
interoperability, and digital rights protocols must close before unguided
singularity systems fragment the public web ecosystem.

Let's ensure the next evolution of the web remains open, secure, and aligned with
human values.
