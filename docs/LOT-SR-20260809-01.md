## WHAT WAS BUILT

### Badge Engine v33 — THE STOIC CODEX

**Word Turn v23** (12 badges): Stoic philosophical vocabulary detection in
journal text. Keywords drawn from classical Stoic canon: Marcus Aurelius's
*Meditations*, Epictetus's *Enchiridion* and *Discourses*, Seneca's
*Letters to Lucilius*. Terms were selected for natural-language journal
fit — words someone actually writes when practicing Stoic philosophy:
memento mori, amor fati, eudaimonia, logos, ataraxia, praxis, askesis,
apatheia, sympatheia, dichotomy of control, kathexis/detachment, hegemonikon.

**Calendar Easter Eggs v21** (3 badges): Three historical Stoic dates —
Marcus Aurelius born March 17, 121 CE; Epictetus born approximately
October 15, ~50 CE; Seneca's death by forced suicide April 16, 65 CE.

**Behavioral v20** (3 badges):
- `stoic_session`: 3+ Stoic Codex v23 words in a single journal entry
- `evening_examination`: Journal entry submitted 21:00–22:59 (the Stoic
  evening review — end-of-day self-examination before sleep)
- `iron_morning`: Check-in before 06:00 local time on 5+ occasions within
  14 days (the Stoic pre-dawn discipline)

**Achievement RPG v21** (6 badges): stoic_entry / stoic_class / stoic_complete /
philosophy_arc (Codex + Calendar) / twenty_three_engines_arc / stoic_opus
(stoic_complete + stoic_session behavioral).

**Mastery Tier v23** (4 badges): Thresholds escalated from v22:
- `iron_discipline`: 1,000+ distinct check-in days (v22: 900+)
- `examined_life`: 200,000+ total journal words (v22: 150,000+)
- `elder_stoic`: Account age >= 8 years (v22: 5 years)
- `twenty_three_registers` [COSMIC]: 1 badge from all 23 Word Turn engines

**Secret Boss v20** (3 badges, hidden): aurelius_codex / epictetus_lamp /
seneca_scroll — triggered by writing the philosophers' names or primary
work titles directly in a journal entry.
