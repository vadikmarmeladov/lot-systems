## 3. v21 — THE ALCHEMIST ENGINE — DESIGN NOTES

### Theme Rationale

v19 (Quantum) and v20 (Navigator) established a pattern of applying sophisticated
conceptual frameworks to self-care. v21 continues this with **Alchemy** —
the pre-scientific tradition of transmutation that is the perfect metaphor for
the LOT mission: the self is not maintained, it is *transformed*.

The alchemist's vocabulary maps cleanly onto self-care:
- **Crucible** → the hard moments you hold through
- **Prima materia** → your raw, unformed starting state
- **Transmutation** → the process of becoming
- **Opus magnum** → the ongoing great work of a life lived intentionally
- **Philosopher's stone** → the final form — not found, but made through process

This is also excellent RPG/fantasy vocabulary: alchemist is a classic RPG class.
