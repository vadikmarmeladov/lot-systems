```
                | DEPLETED | EMPTY             recency of last entry,
                                               stated energy/exhaustion

                                               journal, future planning
                                               language vs. processing
                                               past events

```

**Presence-sensitive output rules:**

```
If GROUNDEDNESS = SCATTERED or ABSENT:
  Engine does NOT present multiple options.
  Engine gives one thing. One instruction. Done.

If CAPACITY = DEPLETED or EMPTY:
  Engine does not ask questions.
  Engine acknowledges and holds. Nothing is asked of the person.

If ORIENTATION = PAST:
  Engine does not push forward.
  Engine stays in the processing mode.
  It does not say "so what's next?" when someone is still inside yesterday.
```

Presence Quality is the session-level read. Soul Vector is the longitudinal read.
Together they tell the engine: *who is this person, and where are they right now?*

### SOUL PHASE 0 — INTAKE (Days 1–30)

**Objective:** Complete a full Soul Vector for each active subscriber.

**Gate:**
```
[ ] Soul Vector generated for all subscribers active 30+ days
[ ] All six intake vectors populated (REACH, RETREAT, LANGUAGE,
    TIMING, INTENSITY, HELP)
[ ] Wound Map: at least one domain flagged or confirmed-absent
[ ] Love Register classified — PRIMARY identified
[ ] COSMO® cleared: Soul Vector contains no clinical language,
    no diagnosis, no pathology labels
[ ] Vadik review: 10 sample Soul Vectors reviewed and approved
```

---

### SOUL PHASE 1 — CALIBRATION (Days 30–90)

**Objective:** Begin calibrating Layer 10 output to Soul Vector signals.
Test whether responses are landing in the correct Love Register.

**Calibration test:**

After each QI·46 response, the subscriber's next journal entry is read.
Signal: does the entry show integration of the response, or deflection from it?

```
INTEGRATION SIGNALS        DEFLECTION SIGNALS
───────────────────        ─────────────────
Returns to topic           Changes subject immediately
Adds detail to topic       Entry is shorter than average
Uses engine language back  No reference to prior session
Reports body settling      Reports restlessness, flat affect
```

Integration rate target: > 70% by Day 90.

---

### SOUL PHASE 2 — RESONANCE (Days 90–180)

**Objective:** The engine begins to speak with the subscriber's own
emotional vocabulary back to them. Not mimicry — resonance.

Resonance means: the subscriber reads the response and does not feel
they are being spoken to by a machine. They feel they are being spoken
to by something that has been listening for a long time.

**Resonance gate:**

The engine passes this phase when the founding cohort (12 subscribers)
returns feedback of YES on > 85% of sessions with notes like:
- "It knew."
- "That was exactly right."
- "I didn't have to explain."
