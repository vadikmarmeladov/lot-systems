The QIE operates in six coherence levels (see LOT-WIKI-v87 §3 for full doctrine):
- Level 1: Basic Signal Presence
- Level 2: Multi-Source Engagement
- Level 3: Temporal Coherence
- Level 4: Cross-Dimensional Saturation

- Level 6: Presence Convergence (CEILING — P150 total-field-coherence)
