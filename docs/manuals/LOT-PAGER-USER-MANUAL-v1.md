## What's in the box

- 1x LOT® Pager (58mm stainless steel puck)
- 1x Charging Plate (40mm x 40mm silver-anodized aluminum)
- 1x USB-C cable (charges the plate, not the Pager directly)
- 1x Quick-start card

## Before you begin

The LOT® Pager has two faces:

- **The polished face** — mirror-finish stainless steel. No buttons, no
  screen. This is the face you leave turned toward the room.
- **The device face** — matte stainless steel, with a small round screen,
  a camera window, and one button marked **COPY**.

There is nothing to charge inside the Pager itself except by setting it on
its Charging Plate — it has no charging port of its own.

## Setup

1. Plug the Charging Plate into any USB-C power source.
2. Set the Pager on the plate, device face up or down — either works, the
   plate charges through either face.
3. Open lot-systems.com on your phone or computer, go to **Settings**
   → **Hardware** → **Pair a Pager**.
4. Follow the on-screen pairing steps. Your Pager's screen will show a
   pairing symbol until setup is complete.
5. Once paired, place the Pager wherever you'll see it — a desk, a
   nightstand, a kitchen counter.

## Using your Pager

**Notifications.** Your Pager receives short messages from your LOT®
Memory Engine — things like "Coffee time!" or "Air's good. Crack a
window." Messages appear on the screen for a short time, then the screen
goes dark again. There is nothing to dismiss, nothing to scroll, and
nothing accumulates while you're away.

**The COPY button.** Press it any time you want a moment logged straight
to your Log tab on lot-systems.com — no phone, no typing. Your Pager
connects on its own and sends the entry. A checkmark on the screen means
it went through; a dash means it's queued and will send once your Pager
reconnects.

**The camera.** The camera only activates when you press COPY, or briefly
and privately on-device to decide whether to wake the screen for you. It
never records continuously, and nothing it sees is stored on the device
past that moment.

## Care

- The polished face shows fingerprints — a soft cloth restores the finish.
  Avoid abrasive cleaners.
- The device face is sealed against splashes (not submersion) — wipe with
  a damp cloth if needed.
- Both faces are 316L stainless steel — dishwasher-safe materials, but the
  Pager itself is not a dishwasher-safe object (it has a battery and a
  screen inside).

## Battery

Your Pager charges wirelessly through its Charging Plate. A full charge
lasts about 5 days under typical use (20-40 notifications a day). There is
no way to check battery level on the device screen by design — pair it
with your account and check status from Settings → Hardware instead.
