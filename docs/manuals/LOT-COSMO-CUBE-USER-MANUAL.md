### 1. What This Is

COSMO® Cube is a small companion tile that sits on your desk, connected to
your Operator account on lot-systems.com. It shows one short line at a
time — a fact, a nudge, a moment the system noticed — and it has one
button: **Copy**.

It does not replace your phone or your screen. It is not a notification
firehose. It shows you one thing when there's one thing worth showing, and
it lets you send that moment into your permanent LOG with a single press.

### 2. In the Box

- 1x COSMO® Cube (4cm x 4cm x 5-8mm, polished stainless front, matte
  stainless back)
- 1x Qi wireless charging dock (USB-C powered)
- 1x USB-C cable
- This manual

### 3. First Setup

1. Place the Cube on the charging dock. The screen will show `PAIR ME`.
2. On your phone or laptop, open **lot-systems.com/pair** while signed in
   to your Operator account.
3. Follow the on-screen instructions to connect to the Cube over
   Bluetooth and enter your WiFi network name and password. This
   information goes directly from your browser to the Cube — LOT servers
   never see your WiFi password.
4. Go to **Settings → Devices** on lot-systems.com and click **Pair a
   COSMO Cube**. A 6-digit code will appear.
5. Enter that code into the pairing page. The Cube will confirm and show
   its first line within a few seconds.

### 4. The Screen

The Cube shows one short line, updated a few times a day — never more
often than roughly every 90 seconds, and only when there's something new
to show. Examples:

- `Coffee time!`
- `Day 214. Steady.`
- `Journal gap: 2 days.`

The screen does not light up, buzz, or interrupt you. It simply holds
whatever it's showing until you look at it — the same way a real pager
does, not the way a phone notification does.

### 5. The Button — Copy

The single button on the back does three things:

| Press | Action |
|---|---|
| **Short press** | Copies the current line into your LOG tab on lot-systems.com as a new entry. |
| **Long press (1 second)** | Skips the wait and pulls a fresh line right now. |
| **Double press** | Takes a quick photo and attaches it to the same LOG entry. |

Every Copy press appears in your LOG tab within a few seconds, tagged so
you can tell it came from the Cube rather than something you typed.

### 6. Charging

Place the Cube face-down... no — place the **polished** face down on the
charging dock (the small ceramic circle in the center of the polished
face is the charging point; the rest of that face is solid stainless).
Full charge takes under an hour. Typical use between charges is several
days, depending on how often the screen updates and how far your Cube is
from your WiFi router.

### 7. Do Not Disturb

Turn the Cube face-down on your desk (screen facing the desk) and it goes
quiet — no screen updates, no network checks — until you turn it back
over.

### 8. Care

- Wipe the polished face with a soft cloth; avoid abrasive cleaners.
- The seam between the two stainless halves has a light splash-resistant
  gasket. The Cube is not submersible.
- There are no user-serviceable parts inside. Do not attempt to open the
  two halves — doing so voids the pairing and may require a full re-pair.

### 9. Resetting

Hold the button for 10 seconds while the Cube is off the charger. The
screen will show `RESET` and the Cube returns to its out-of-box, unpaired
state. Repeat Section 3 to set it up again.

### 10. Firmware Updates

The Cube checks for firmware updates automatically and applies them
overnight when charging. You do not need to do anything. The current
firmware version is shown on lot-systems.com under Settings → Devices.

### 11. Support

Questions about your Cube: reach LOT Systems support through your
Operator account on lot-systems.com. Include your device name (shown in
Settings → Devices) so support can look up your specific unit's pairing
and firmware state.
