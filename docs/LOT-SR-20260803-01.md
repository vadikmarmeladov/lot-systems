## HONEST FINDINGS DISCLOSED IN THE PLAN (NOT SMOOTHED OVER)

- The brief's 5mm total height (point 4) does not fit a camera + display +
  battery + Qi coil + button stack at today's commercial part thicknesses —
  stacked at ~5.1mm before the camera module is even added. v1.0 ships at
  6.8mm; 5mm is retained as a named, gated v2.0 target (chip-on-board
  camera, thinner cell), not silently rounded.
- 316L stainless steel (points 3, 17, 18) meaningfully degrades Qi coupling
  behind solid metal. Resolved with a 22mm Macor (machinable glass-ceramic)
  charging-window insert on the charging face, flush-polished — disclosed
  as a real constraint with a named mitigation, not glossed over.
- The camera's privacy design is stated as a firmware/build-level
  guarantee (no code path from camera buffer to radio driver, enforced by
  a standing code-review rule) rather than a policy promise, matching the
  brand's existing "this is not surveillance" register
  (`docs/corporate/LOT_ROBOTICS_COSMO.md`).
- FCC/CE radio certification and UN38.3 battery shipping certification are
  named as unscoped, unbudgeted blockers before any of the 100 units can
  ship to hands outside controlled internal testing — not waived by
  hitting the engineering gates.
- The predecessor Qi receiver part first considered (STWLC33JR) was found
  NRND (no longer manufactured) during the 2026-08-03 sourcing pass and
  swapped for the current STWLC03 before it entered the BOM.
