## Welcome

Your COSMO® CIA Device is a physical companion for your LOT Memory Engine. It receives personal, context-aware notifications from `lot-systems.com` — messages crafted by your AI companion that knows your routines, preferences, and rhythms.

The device is intentionally simple:  
- A **screen** to show you messages  
- A **camera** to capture moments  
- One **button** to say "received"  
- Wireless charging — no cables, no ports

## Setup (First Use)

**Step 1 — Charge**  
Place the device on the charging dock (logo side down on the dock pad). The screen shows a charging indicator. Charge for 90 minutes before first use.

**Step 2 — Connect to LOT**  
1. Open `lot-systems.com` on your phone or computer  
2. Go to **Settings → Devices → Add CIA Device**  
3. The screen shows "Pair Mode" — a BLE signal activates for 5 minutes  
4. Tap **Pair** on the LOT website — the device connects to your WiFi automatically  
5. The screen shows "Connected ✓"  

**Step 3 — Receive your first notification**  
Your LOT Memory Engine sends notifications based on your routines. Within 24 hours of connection, you'll receive your first message.

## Daily Use

### Receiving a Notification

The screen activates and displays your message:

```
  ◉ LOT

  Coffee time!

  09:14  ●●●○○
```

The message stays on screen for 8 seconds, then dims. The device returns to sleep.

### The Copy Button

Press the circular button on the front to acknowledge a notification. This:
- Sends a **"received"** signal to the Log tab at `lot-systems.com`
- Lets your AI companion know you acted on the message
- Feeds back into your Memory Story over time

**Button feedback:**
- White pulse = button press registered
- Green pulse = server confirmed
- Amber pulse = queued (WiFi unavailable, will sync later)

### Camera

Point the camera (the small circular aperture next to the button) at an object and press the button **twice quickly** (double-tap) to capture a photo. The image is uploaded to your LOT Memory Story.

## Charging

1. Place device on the dock with the **mirror-polished back facing down**  
2. The 4 magnets in the dock snap the device into alignment automatically  
3. Screen shows battery percentage while charging  
4. Full charge: approximately 90 minutes  
5. Typical battery life: 48 hours with normal notification volume  

**The dock connects to any USB-C power source (5V/2A minimum).**

## Sensor Data

Your CIA Device automatically measures your environment and shares it with your LOT Memory Engine:

| Sensor | What it measures |
|--------|------------------|
| Temperature | Room temperature (°C / °F) |
| Humidity | Air humidity (%) |
| Air pressure | Barometric pressure (hPa) |
| Air quality (VOC) | Indoor air quality index |
| Motion | Steps, activity level |

This data helps your Memory Engine make smarter, more relevant suggestions.

## Settings (via lot-systems.com)

Go to **Settings → Devices → CIA Device** to configure:

- **Notification volume** — how many notifications per day
- **Quiet hours** — screen stays off during these hours
- **Display brightness** — auto or manual
- **Screen timeout** — how long messages stay on (default: 8s)
- **Sensor upload interval** — default: every 5 minutes
- **Device name** — personalize your device label

## Factory Reset

Press and hold the Copy button for **10 seconds** until the screen shows "Resetting…". This clears WiFi credentials and device token. You'll need to re-pair via `lot-systems.com/device/setup`.

## Care

- Clean with a dry or slightly damp microfiber cloth
- The mirror-polished back will show fingerprints — wipe gently
- Do not submerge in water (IP54 = splash resistant only)
- Keep away from strong magnetic fields (may affect Qi charging alignment)
- Operating temperature: 0°C – 45°C
