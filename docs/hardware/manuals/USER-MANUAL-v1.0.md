## What This Device Does

Your LOT Computer node is a physical extension of your LOT account
at lot-systems.com. It sits on your desk, charges wirelessly, and
shows you autonomous notifications from your LOT intelligence system.

When you press the button, it logs the moment to your LOT account.

That is all it does. It does it perfectly.

## First-Time Setup

### Step 1 — Charge

Place the device on the LOT Charge Base (mirror-polish side down).
The pad LED turns amber. Charge for 45 minutes before first use.

### Step 2 — Open your LOT account

Go to lot-systems.com and log in.
Navigate to Settings → Devices → Add Physical Node.

### Step 3 — Pair

On the Devices page, click "Pair New Device". Your phone/computer
will connect via Bluetooth to the device (it broadcasts "LOT-HW-001").

Follow the on-screen steps to enter your WiFi password.
The device display shows "Connected ✓" when setup is complete.

### Step 4 — Receive your first notification

Within a minute, your LOT system will push a welcome notification.
The display wakes and shows the message.

---

## Daily Use

**Receiving notifications:**
The display wakes automatically when a notification arrives.
It shows the message for 30 seconds, then sleeps.

**Pressing the Copy button:**
Press the button on Side B to acknowledge a notification and
send a log entry to your LOT Log tab.

The display shows "✓ Logged to LOT" for 2 seconds.

**Charging:**
Place the device mirror-side down on the LOT Charge Base.
Charge time: ~45 minutes. Battery life: 3–7 days.

## Firmware Updates

Updates are delivered automatically over WiFi.
When an update is available:
1. Device display shows "Updating..." (do not remove from charger)
2. Update takes ~60 seconds
3. Device restarts and shows current firmware version
