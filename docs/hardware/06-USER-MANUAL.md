# COSMO® Cube — User Manual (v1.0)

*Source document for `docs/hardware/pdf/LOT-COSMO-CUBE-USER-MANUAL-v1.pdf`.*

---

## What it is

COSMO® Cube is a small stainless-steel LOT® terminal that sits on your
desk. It shows one short line from lot-systems.com when the system has
something for you — a badge, a reminder, a weather note — and it has one
button: **Copy**.

It is not a phone. It has no feed, no unread count, and no apps. One line,
one button.

---

## In the box

- 1x COSMO® Cube (40 x 40 x 5mm, stainless steel)
- 1x wireless charging dock
- 1x quick-start card with your device's pairing code

---

## Setup

1. Place COSMO® Cube polished-side-down on the charging dock.
2. The screen shows a 6-character pairing code.
3. On your phone or computer, go to **lot-systems.com/hardware/pair** and
   sign in to your LOT® account.
4. Enter the pairing code shown on the device.
5. Enter your Wi-Fi network name and password when prompted.
6. The screen briefly shows the LOT® wordmark, then goes to standby.
   Setup is complete.

---

## Using it

**When a notice arrives**, one line appears on the screen — for example
*"Coffee time!"* or *"Badge unlocked — check your Log."*

**Press the button (short press)** to copy the notice to your Log tab at
lot-systems.com and clear the screen. Your Log tab will show the entry
within a few seconds, the same as anything you log from the app.

**Hold the button (2 seconds)** to attach a photo to that Log entry using
the onboard camera. This is off by default — turn it on in **Settings >
Hardware** at lot-systems.com if you want it.

**Charging:** rest the device polished-side-down on the dock. A full
charge from empty takes a few hours; the device sips power between
notices, so daily topping-up is typically enough.

---

## Privacy

- The camera is unpowered except during an explicit long-press capture,
  or during an optional presence check you can turn on or off yourself.
- No raw camera image ever leaves the device unless you trigger a
  snapshot with a long press.
- Weather readings (temperature, humidity, pressure) are used to give
  the AI a hyperlocal read of your desk, not tracked or sold.
- You can see and revoke your device's access anytime from **Settings >
  Hardware** at lot-systems.com — revoking is instant.

---

## Care

- The polished face is stainless steel — it will show fingerprints like
  any mirror-finish metal. A soft cloth restores the shine.
- The device is splash-resistant, not waterproof — do not submerge it.
- No user-serviceable parts inside; do not attempt to open the shell.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to try |
|---|---|
| Screen stays blank | Confirm it's seated correctly on the charging dock; check for a low-battery glyph |
| Pairing code won't accept | Codes expire after a few minutes — press and hold the button 5 seconds to generate a new one |
| Notices stop arriving | Check Wi-Fi at home; check **Settings > Hardware** to confirm the device isn't revoked |
| Copy doesn't appear in Log | Check your internet connection — presses made while offline sync automatically once reconnected |
