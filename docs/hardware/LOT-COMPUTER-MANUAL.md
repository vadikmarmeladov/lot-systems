# LOT® Computer
## Quick Start Manual — v1.0 Pilot

---

### What it is

A small stainless steel object for your desk. One face is mirror-polished
stainless — that's the charging face. The other face carries a screen, a
small camera, and one button marked **COPY**.

It receives short messages from lot-systems.com — "Coffee time!" and the
like — and lets you send a moment back with a single press.

---

### In the box

- 1x LOT® Computer (40mm x 40mm x 5mm)
- 1x Wireless charging puck
- This manual

---

### 1. Charge it

Place the device mirror-face-down on the charging puck. There is no
port and no cable that connects to the device itself — only the puck
plugs in. A full charge from empty takes a few hours; the device is
designed to live on the puck most of the time, not to be carried like a
phone.

### 2. Pair it

On first power-up, the screen shows a short pairing code. At
lot-systems.com, go to **Settings → Devices → Pair a device** and enter
the code. Pairing is one-time — the device remembers your account after
this step.

### 3. Let it sit

Once paired, place the device wherever you'd notice it without it
demanding attention — beside a keyboard, under a monitor, on a shelf at
eye level. When lot-systems.com has something short and timely to tell
you, it appears on the screen. The screen holds that message with the
display technology it uses even if the device loses power, so you won't
miss it by glancing over later.

### 4. Use COPY

Press the button once to capture the moment — what you're looking at,
what you're doing, a thought worth keeping. The device takes a quiet
photo and sends it, with a timestamp, straight to your **Log** tab at
lot-systems.com. There's nothing to open on the device itself — check
your Log tab the way you always do.

One press. One meaning. That's the whole gesture vocabulary.

---

### Care

- The mirror face will show fingerprints — that's the charging contact
  face, meant to be handled and set back down often. A soft cloth
  restores the finish.
- The matte face (screen, camera, button) should not be submerged or
  run under water — the device is not rated for immersion.
- There is no user-serviceable part inside. The two stainless halves
  are not meant to be separated by the owner.

---

### If something's not right

| Symptom | Try this |
|---|---|
| Screen shows nothing after charging | Confirm the mirror face is flat against the puck — a slight misalignment can prevent charging |
| COPY press doesn't appear in the Log tab | Check the device is still paired under Settings → Devices; re-pair if it shows as "revoked" |
| No notifications ever arrive | Confirm the device shows "active," not "provisioning," under Settings → Devices |

---

### What LOT® Computer is not

It is not a phone. It has no apps, no browser, and no notifications
beyond the short messages lot-systems.com sends it. That's deliberate —
the whole point of the object is to say less, not more.
