## What COSMO® SLATE does

SLATE is a small steel object that sits on your desk and shows you one
short message at a time — sent from your LOT® Systems account
(lot-systems.com) when there's something worth knowing. It has one button.
Pressing it sends a signal straight to the **Log** tab on your LOT account,
the same as if you'd typed a log entry yourself.

That's the whole device. One line in, one button out.

---

## Setup

1. **Charge it.** Place SLATE face-down on the charging puck. A soft glow
   under the base confirms it's charging.
2. **Power on.** Lift SLATE off the puck once charged. The display wakes
   and shows a short pairing code.
3. **Connect Wi-Fi.** On your phone or laptop, open Bluetooth settings,
   find "COSMO SLATE," and follow the prompt to hand it your home Wi-Fi
   network. You only do this once.
4. **Pair with your account.** On lot-systems.com, go to
   **Settings → Hardware → Pair a device**, and enter the code shown on
   SLATE's screen.
5. **Done.** SLATE will show "Paired" briefly, then go quiet until it has
   something to say.

## Using SLATE

**The screen.** SLATE stays blank — a polished steel mirror — until your
LOT account has something worth telling you. When it does, one line
appears: something like *"Coffee time!"* or *"Memory question ready."*
It stays until you either act on it or a new message replaces it.

**The button.** One press sends **Copy** — a signal that shows up in your
**Log** tab on lot-systems.com, timestamped, exactly like a note you wrote
yourself. Use it however makes sense to you: to mark that you saw the
message, that you're stepping away, or just to check in.

**Turning it over.** The polished side has no controls — it's meant to sit
face-up as a quiet object until you need it. Flip it to the working side
(camera, screen, button) any time.

## Charging

SLATE charges wirelessly on its puck. A full charge lasts roughly 4-5 days
of typical use (6-10 messages a day). There's no cable to plug into the
device itself — just set it back on the puck when the display starts
running low.

---

## Weather awareness

SLATE has a small onboard sensor that reads temperature, humidity, and
air pressure near your desk. This feeds into the messages it shows you —
for example, a heads-up about rain — and into your LOT account's broader
picture of your day. It never leaves your desk data anywhere public.

---

## Privacy — what the camera does and doesn't do

SLATE has a small camera on its working face. It is used only to sense
whether someone is at the desk and how bright the room is, so messages
show up when you're actually there to see them. **It does not record or
transmit video.** No raw image ever leaves the device — only a simple
"someone's here" / "room is bright" signal, sent alongside the weather
data.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| Screen stays blank | Confirm it's charged — place on the puck for 10 minutes, then check |
| Won't connect to Wi-Fi | Re-run step 3 of setup; confirm your network is 2.4GHz (SLATE doesn't use 5GHz Wi-Fi) |
| Button press doesn't show in Log tab | Check Settings → Hardware — the device should show "Paired" and "Last seen: recently" |
| Battery draining faster than expected | Return it to the charging puck more often; report the pattern in Settings → Hardware → Feedback so a future firmware update can improve it |
