# COSMO® Cube — Quick Start Guide

**A hardware computer connected to lot-systems.com.**

---

## 1. What's in the Box

- 1× COSMO® Cube (40mm × 40mm × 5mm, stainless steel)
- 1× LOT® Wireless Charging Pad + USB-C cable
- 1× Quick Start card (this guide)
- 1× QR code card → full documentation

---

## 2. Which Side Is Which

**Mirror-polished side (back):** This is the charging face. Place it down on
the charging pad. The LOT® wordmark is engraved here.

**Satin side (front):** This is the display, camera, and Copy button. This
side faces up on your desk.

---

## 3. First Charge

1. Place the charging pad on your desk and connect it via USB-C.
2. Set the Cube mirror-side-down, centered on the pad.
3. The status LED breathes white while charging, and turns solid green at 100%.
4. A full charge from empty takes about 2.5 hours.

---

## 4. Pairing to Your LOT Account

1. Open lot-systems.com and go to **My Devices → Add Device**.
2. The site shows a short pairing code.
3. On first boot, your Cube shows a QR code on its display — scan it, or
   enter the pairing code directly.
4. Once paired, the Cube's display shows the current time and today's weather.

---

## 5. Using the Copy Button

Press the button on the front face at any time. Your Cube will:

1. Read its temperature, humidity, pressure, light, and orientation sensors.
2. Send that snapshot to lot-systems.com.
3. Show "Logged." on the display for 3 seconds.
4. Add an entry to your **Log tab**, tagged **[COSMO® Cube]**.

There is no wrong time to press it. Every press is a deliberate, timestamped
note about where you were and what the world around you looked like.

---

## 6. Notifications

Your COSMO® Cube receives short messages from your LOT AI — things like
"Coffee time!" or a reminder about an upcoming event. These appear on the
display for up to 30 seconds, then the screen returns to the clock. There is
no chime, no vibration, no push banner — just a message, present until you
look.

---

## 7. Care

- Wipe the mirror-polished side with a soft microfiber cloth only. Do not use
  abrasive cleaners — they will scratch the finish.
- The Cube is splash-resistant (IP54), not waterproof. Do not submerge it.
- Operating range: 0°C to 45°C.

---
