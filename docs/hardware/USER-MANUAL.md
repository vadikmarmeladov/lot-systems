## What COSMO Does

**Receives** — Autonomous notifications from lot-systems.com appear on its screen. Custom reminders, Memory Engine prompts, weather shifts, personal insights.

Examples:
- "Coffee time!"
- "Memory check-in"
- "New insight: Morning person"
- "Rain arriving in 2 hours"

**Sends** — When you press the Copy button, COSMO sends a signal back to the Log tab in your LOT System. You can use this to confirm, acknowledge, or simply say "I saw this."

**Senses** — COSMO reads temperature, humidity, air quality, and ambient light, and sends those readings to your LOT System as ambient context.

## First Setup

### Step 1 — Charge

Place COSMO on the charging pad (back side down). Orange LED = charging. Green LED = full. First charge takes ~2.5 hours.

### Step 2 — Pair with your LOT System

1. Open lot-systems.com on your phone or computer
2. Go to **Settings → Hardware**
3. Tap **"Add COSMO device"**
4. A QR code appears on screen
5. Press and hold the **Copy button for 3 seconds** — COSMO enters pairing mode and shows "Point at QR"
6. Hold COSMO facing your screen so the camera can read the QR
7. COSMO connects automatically

Setup is complete when the screen shows:  
**"Connected. Welcome, [Your Name]!"**

### Step 3 — Your first notification

Visit lot-systems.com → **Log tab** → tap the **bell icon** → type a message and send it to your COSMO. It should appear within 30 seconds.

## Charging

### Daily charging

Place COSMO back-side-down on the Qi pad. Charging begins automatically.

- Orange LED = charging
- Green LED = full (100%)
- Charge time: ~2.5 hours (380 mAh battery, 5W Qi)
- Battery life: ~48 hours standby / ~8 hours with screen active

### LED behavior while charging

| LED color | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Orange steady | Charging (battery < 80%) |
| Orange slow pulse | Charging (battery 80–99%) |
| Green steady | Fully charged |

### USB-C charging

COSMO can also charge via the USB-C port on the bottom edge (standard 5V/1A). Use this for firmware updates or travel charging without the pad.

## Sending Notifications to COSMO (from LOT Site)

1. Go to lot-systems.com → **Settings → Hardware**
2. Select your COSMO device
3. Tap **"Send notification"**
4. Type message (max 40 characters)
5. Choose haptic: soft / buzz / triple pulse
6. Tap **Send** — delivered within 30 seconds

Notifications can also come automatically from:
- Your LOT Memory Engine (daily question prompts)
- AI-detected insights from your Quantum Intent Engine
- Weather service integration
- Custom scheduled reminders (set in LOT Settings)

## Firmware Updates

COSMO updates its own firmware automatically — once per day, when connected to Wi-Fi, it checks for updates from lot-systems.com.

When an update is available:
- COSMO downloads and installs it overnight (or next time on charger)
- Screen shows: "Updating firmware — please wait"
- Update takes ~2 minutes
- Device restarts with new firmware

You can also trigger a manual update via LOT Settings → Hardware → [your device] → "Check for update".

## Care & Cleaning

- **Front face:** Wipe with soft microfiber cloth. Avoid abrasives.
- **Mirror back:** Polishes easily with any optical cloth. Fingerprints clean with breath + cloth.
- **Charging pad:** Wipe with dry cloth.
- **Water:** COSMO is splash-resistant (IP52) — brief splashes OK; do not submerge.
- **Temperature:** Operate between 0°C and 40°C. Charge between 10°C and 35°C.
- **Magnets:** Keep away from strong magnets (may affect haptic motor and charging coil).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Screen blank | Press Copy button once to wake. Check battery (place on charger). |
| No notifications appearing | Check Wi-Fi: Settings → Hardware → device status. Verify lot-systems.com account is active. |
| Copy button not logging | Verify device is online (blue LED pulse). Check LOT Log tab for errors. |
| Not charging wirelessly | Center device on pad. Remove thick cases/objects between device and pad. |
| Won't pair (QR scan fails) | Ensure screen brightness on computer is 100%. Hold COSMO 10–20cm from screen. Re-generate QR in LOT Settings (5-min expiry). |
| Sensor readings wrong | BME688 needs ~30min warm-up after first use for accurate gas readings. Temperature may read +1–2°C high immediately after charging. |

## Safety

- Do not disassemble COSMO. No user-serviceable parts inside.
- Do not charge in temperatures below 10°C or above 35°C.
- The LiPo battery is a fire hazard if punctured or crushed — handle with care.
- Do not expose to liquids beyond brief splashes.
- Keep away from children under 3 (small device, potential choking hazard).
