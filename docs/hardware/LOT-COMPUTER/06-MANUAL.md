# LOT COMPUTER
## Quick Reference Manual — Rev A (Pilot, 100 units)

Made in the USA. LOT® Founded 7 April 2016. © 2025-2026 LOT Systems.

---

### What it is

LOT COMPUTER is a small stainless steel object that sits on your desk. It
has two faces. One is a mirror. The other has a camera, a screen, a
sensor grille, and one button.

It does not run apps. It does not need to be opened. It receives one line
at a time from your LOT account and shows it on the screen — the way a
pager used to print a page. Then it goes quiet again.

---

### In the box

- LOT COMPUTER unit (42 × 42 × 11mm, polished/matte 304 stainless)
- Wireless charging dock (matching stainless puck)
- This manual

No cable is required for daily use. A concealed USB-C service port exists
under Face B for factory setup only.

---

### Setup

1. Place LOT COMPUTER on the charging dock. The status glyph appears on
   the instrument face when it has power.
2. On lot-systems.com, open **Settings → Devices → Pair a LOT COMPUTER**.
3. A QR code appears on your screen. Hold LOT COMPUTER's camera face
   toward it, roughly 15–20cm away, until the glyph confirms.
4. Pairing is complete. The device is now bound to your account only.

---

### Daily use

**The screen.** Blank by default. When your LOT account has something to
tell you — "Coffee time!", a weather note, a reminder — one line appears.
It stays until the next line replaces it. There is nothing to scroll,
nothing to dismiss.

**The COPY button.** Press it any time you want to mark the moment. It
writes a single entry to your Log on lot-systems.com — the same Log your
web app already keeps — with the time, your local temperature and
humidity, and the device's battery level. Nothing is typed. The gesture
itself is the entry.

**The sensor grille.** Reads temperature, humidity, pressure, and whether
someone is nearby — nothing is recorded locally, and nothing leaves the
device except through the same two paths above (a screen update in, a
COPY press out).

**The camera.** Used only for setup pairing and, if you turn it on in
Settings, an optional one-shot "Visual Log" — a single photo, on request,
uploaded once. It is off otherwise. It never streams and never records
video.

---

### Charging

Set LOT COMPUTER face-down or face-up on its dock — either face; the coil
is centered so orientation doesn't matter. A full charge from empty takes
several hours depending on dock wattage; typical daily top-up is much
shorter. There is no on/off switch — the device sleeps between
notifications and wakes itself.

---

### Care

- Face A (the mirror) is polished stainless. Clean with a soft cloth; avoid
  abrasives, which will dull the polish permanently.
- Face B (the instrument face) is bead-blasted and more forgiving of
  everyday handling.
- The device is splash-resistant (IP54 target), not submersible. Do not
  immerse it.
- No user-serviceable parts inside. The four closure screws are for
  factory service only.

---

### If it stops responding

1. Confirm it has charge (place on the dock; wait for the status glyph).
2. Confirm your home Wi-Fi is online — LOT COMPUTER needs your network to
   reach lot-systems.com; it does not use cellular data.
3. Re-pair from **Settings → Devices** if it was moved to a new network
   or account.
4. If none of the above resolves it, contact support@lot-systems.com with
   the device ID printed on the underside of the charging dock
   (`LOT-COMPUTER-00xx`).

---

### What this device does not do

It does not listen. There is no microphone. It does not watch — the
camera fires only for setup and an explicit opt-in photo you request. It
does not need an app open to work, and it does not work at all without
your LOT account behind it — it is a terminal for your account, not a
standalone gadget.
