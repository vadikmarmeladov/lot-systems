## What Is COSMO® CIA?

COSMO® CIA is your personal AI notification pager from LOT Systems. It shows notifications from your LOT account — messages, reminders, and AI-generated insights — on its small screen. Press the COPY button to instantly log a moment to your LOT Log tab.

---

## 1. First-Time Setup

### Step 1 — Get a Registration Code

1. Log in to **lot-systems.com**
2. Go to **Settings → Devices**
3. Click **"Add New Device"**
4. A code like **`A3F2C9D1`** appears — keep it open

### Step 2 — Power On

1. Remove COSMO® CIA from its box
2. Place it on the charging pad (polished side down)
3. Wait 5 seconds — the screen lights up with: **"COSMO® CIA"**

### Step 3 — Connect to Wi-Fi

1. On the device screen you'll see: **"Pair via LOT App"**
2. On your phone, open the LOT app or visit lot-systems.com on mobile
3. Go to **Settings → Devices → Connect via Bluetooth**
4. Select your COSMO® CIA from the list
5. Enter your **Wi-Fi network name and password**
6. The device connects and shows: **"✓ Connected"**

### Step 4 — Enter Registration Code

1. On the device screen, use the COPY button to navigate (short press = confirm)
2. Select **"Enter Code"**
3. Enter the 8-character code from Step 1 (e.g., `A3F2C9D1`)
4. The screen shows: **"✓ Registered to [Your Name]"**

Setup is complete. Your COSMO® CIA is now linked to your LOT account.

## 3. The COPY Button

The COPY button is on the front face (Side B), below the screen.

| Press | Action |
|---|---|
| **Short press** (< 1 second) | Log current moment to your LOT Log tab |
| **Long press** (3 seconds) | Dismiss current notification |
| **Double press** | Turn screen on/off (saves battery) |

When you short-press COPY:
1. The device vibrates briefly ·
2. The screen flashes **"✓ Copied to Log"**
3. A new entry appears in your LOT Log tab at lot-systems.com with the current time, temperature, humidity, and air quality

---

## 4. Notifications

Notifications are sent from lot-systems.com or generated by the LOT AI engine. They appear on the screen automatically, with no action required on your part.

Notification types:

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Reminder | "Coffee time! ☕" |
| Alert | "Meeting in 15 minutes" |
| AI insight | "Your IAQ is low — open a window 🌿" |
| System | "Battery at 20% — please charge" |

To dismiss a notification: **long-press the COPY button** (3 seconds).

## 5. Charging

1. Place COSMO® CIA polished-side down on the charging pad
2. The device aligns with the magnets and charges automatically
3. The edge LED shows status:

| LED colour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Red (pulsing) | Charging |
| Orange | Almost full |
| Green (steady) | Fully charged |
| Off | Not on charger |

Battery life: ~4 hours continuous use · ~23 days standby.

---

## 6. Sensors

Your COSMO® CIA continuously measures the environment using its Bosch BME688 AI-grade sensor:

| Reading | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Temperature** (°C) | Ambient air temperature |
| **Humidity** (% RH) | Relative humidity |
| **Pressure** (hPa) | Barometric pressure (useful for weather) |
| **IAQ Score** (0–500) | Indoor Air Quality: 0–50 = excellent, 51–100 = good, 101–150 = moderate, >151 = poor |

IAQ readings are sent to your LOT account every 5 minutes and can be viewed in your Log tab.

## 7. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Screen is blank | Press COPY button once to wake; if no response, charge for 30 minutes |
| "No connection" on screen | Check Wi-Fi password; re-pair via LOT App Settings |
| COPY button not logging | Check lot-systems.com is reachable; ensure device is registered |
| Device gets warm | Normal during charging; if hot, remove from charger and contact support |
| Stuck on "Connecting…" | Hold COPY for 10 seconds to reset Wi-Fi; re-pair |

## 9. Care & Safety

- Do not submerge in water (IP52 = splash only)
- Do not expose to temperatures above 45°C (e.g., direct sunlight, car dashboard)
- Do not attempt to open the device — no user-serviceable parts
- Use only the supplied charging pad or a Qi-certified charger
- If the device is dropped and the screen cracks, contact support
